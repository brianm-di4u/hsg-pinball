Spa CityPinball

Pinball table. Keep the ball in play with the left and right flippers, hit targets to light bathhouses and start modes, and score as much as you can. Hold Space or the Launch button to pull the plunger and release to fire. Z and slash, or Shift, flip; X, period and up arrow nudge; Escape pauses. On a touch screen, tap the left or right half of the table to flip. Callouts, the ball number and your score at the end of each ball are announced.
