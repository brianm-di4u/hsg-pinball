# Critic A (critic-claude): loop 7

## Verdict

Spa City Pinball now looks like a real digital pinball table and not a browser toy, but it is not yet at the level of a shipped Zen table. The painted upper playfield, the deco terrazzo lower field and the backglass are clearly by one hand. The DMD has character, with the horse, the Arlington towers and the vapor icons. The Mountain Tower, the Arlington and the Vapors sign all read at a glance. This loop's glare work is visible and real: satin bumpers, a tamer wizard star and a smaller spinner flare.

The table still looks like assembled parts, not a photographed machine:
- Bathhouses stand on cream box plinths.
- Scoop and lock walls are flat grey bars.
- Flippers are blank white extrusions.
- Slingshots have no visible rubber.
- The apron is an empty purple slab with two cream cards.

Some glare remains: the spinner edge still blooms into a white bar, and the lock wall rim blows out. On desktop the ball is still a 12 px dot on a busy painting. Phone landscape wastes most of the screen. Every category sits just under its gate, and none clears it convincingly.

**Total: 59.5 / 90.**

## Scores

### Art: 6.5
- **Upper field:** `desktop/R02-serve` and `desktop/R03-play` show a lush painted grotto and cascade behind the bumpers and lanes. The lower half has a deco terrazzo fan that frames the 2X–5X inserts and the flippers well (`desktop/C03-flippers`).
- **Backglass:** `desktop/R02-serve` (left column) is a strong painted night scene of the Row, the Arlington, the tower and neon, in the same hand as the playfield.
- **Below a 7:**
  - The upper painting is a generic garden that ignores the layout: shrubs and rocks run straight under the lanes and bumpers (`desktop/C02-springs`).
  - Shot arrows are flat vector shapes in four unrelated colours, pasted on top of the art (`desktop/C04-vapors`, the blue and orange arrows at the bottom).
  - Mid-field between the Vapors and the ramp foot is empty terrazzo (`desktop/R03-play`).
  - The apron is a blank purple slab with two plain cream cards (`desktop/R02-serve`, bottom).

### Toys: 6.5
- **Reads well:**
  - Mountain Tower: lattice legs and an octagonal cab (`desktop/C05-tower`).
  - The Arlington: twin red-roofed towers (`desktop/C06-arlington`).
  - Quapaw: mosaic dome; Fordyce: skylight (`desktop/C06-arlington`).
  - The Vapors: neon sign (`desktop/C04-vapors`).
  - Bumpers: satin teal with 143°F caps (`desktop/C02-springs`).
  - Ramp mouth: now stainless blades (`desktop/C05-tower`).
  - Ball: chrome.
- **Weak:**
  - Bathhouses sit on cream boxes, like toy blocks, and have flat facade decals with no bevels or wear (`desktop/C01-row`).
  - Scoop and lock walls are flat grey extrusions (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C06-arlington`).
  - Flippers are featureless white bats with a red stripe and no rubber texture (`desktop/C03-flippers`).
  - Slingshots look like painted panels, with no rubber ring around them (`desktop/C03-flippers`).
  - The ramp floor is a flat frosted grey with visible panel seams, not clear plastic over art (`desktop/C05-tower`).

### Lighting: 6.5
- **Works:**
  - Lit inserts bloom and unlit ones stay dark (2X/3X against 4X/5X in `desktop/C03-flippers`).
  - The GI is warm.
  - Wizard mode dims the GI and lights every insert, a real mood shift (`desktop/R08-wizard`, `mobile/R08-wizard`).
  - A bumper hit visibly brightens the bumper body (`strips/S02-bumpers` frame 1).
- **Remaining defects:**
  - The spinner still flares into a hard white bar (`desktop/R03-play` near 478,270; `mobile/R03-play` near 98,770).
  - The Arlington lock wall top edge blows out white (`desktop/C06-arlington`).
  - The top-left of `desktop/C04-vapors` blows out to white.
  - Bumper base rings still glow white-hot (`desktop/C02-springs`).
  - The new contact shadows are subtle (`desktop/C01-row`).
  - The table is lit evenly overall, not "a lit machine in a dark room".

### Animation: 6.5
- **`strips/S01-flip`:** frame 2 catches the left flipper mid-swing, and the ball leaves cleanly up the centre over frames 3–7.
- **`strips/S02-bumpers`:** the ball now strikes the upper-right bumper. The bumper lights (frames 1, 2, 4, 6) and steam rises.
- **`strips/S03-multiball`:** a light show sweeps the inserts and the GI shifts.
- **Missing:**
  - No visible skirt drop on a bumper hit.
  - No ball trail or motion cue.
  - No camera reaction.
  - `strips/S03-multiball` is badly framed. The flippers are cropped off, the table is pushed left with black on the right, and almost no ball is visible during a "multiball launch" (only frames 5–7 show one ball in the shooter lane and wireform).

### UI: 6.5
- **Works:**
  - The DMD has real character: dot-matrix icons for Derby, the Arlington and the Vapors (`desktop/R06-multiball`, `desktop/R07-mode`, `mobile/R08-wizard`).
  - The side panel shows score, ball and mode progress.
  - The new six-lamp mode grid is legible (`desktop/R07-mode`).
  - The title menu is clean (`desktop/R01-title`).
- **Weak:**
  - Menus are generic rounded web buttons in purple and gold, not deco styled (`desktop/R01-title`, `mobile/R01-title`).
  - The mode grid has no legend for gold against purple-outline states.
  - In `desktop/R08-wizard` the grid shows only Soak won during the wizard, which the help screen says needs all six. The HUD contradicts the game state.
  - The right column has about 220 px of dead space under Menu (`desktop/R03-play`).
  - On the phone the mode timer and progress ("Derby · 18/100 · 45s" on desktop) disappear (`mobile/R07-mode`).

### Typography: 6.5
- **Works:**
  - A slab-serif display face with a neon glow for the title and headers (`desktop/R01-title`, `desktop/R10-scores`).
  - A clean dot-matrix DMD font that reads at 390 px (`mobile/R03-play`).
  - Crisp playfield lettering at close-up range (`desktop/C01-row`: THE ARLINGTON, LOCK, the bathhouse plates).
- **Weak:**
  - Body text looks like the system UI sans, not a chosen face (`desktop/R09-help`).
  - The help list is cut off mid-line ("meter for an extra ball") behind the Back bar (`desktop/R09-help`, `mobile/R09-help`).
  - Mode-insert and bathhouse labels are about 5 px and unreadable at the playing view (`desktop/R02-serve`; `mobile/R02-serve` at SOAK, DERBY and the bathhouse plates).
  - The apron cards use a default-looking serif (`desktop/R02-serve`).

### Readability: 6.5
- **Works:**
  - Lit and unlit inserts are clearly different.
  - Mode shots light as large coloured arrows (`desktop/R07-mode`).
  - With satin bumpers, the bumper highlights no longer pose as balls (`mobile/R06-multiball`).
  - On the phone the ball reads well, a chrome sphere with a dark rim (`mobile/R07-mode`, on the wireform).
- **Desktop problem:**
  - The ball is still about 12 px inside a table about 430 px wide.
  - In `desktop/R03-play` it sits on the MOUNTAIN TOWER plate and the terrazzo and is easy to miss.
  - In `desktop/R06-multiball` the top ball is lost against the arch.
  - The skill-shot cue in `desktop/R02-serve` is one small lamp in the top-right lanes, and "LANE A" on the DMD is cryptic.

### Mobile: 6.5
- **Portrait is good:**
  - The DMD is large at the top and the table fills the width.
  - The ☰ and Launch buttons sit in the apron band, clear of the ball's area (`mobile/R02-serve`, `mobile/R06-multiball`).
  - The menus fit (`mobile/R09-help`, `mobile/R10-scores`).
- **Landscape is poor:** `mobile/R11-landscape` shows a small, steeply tilted table floating in about 40% of the width. There is a black void about 330 px wide on the left and more black between the table and the HUD.
- **Also:** mode progress is missing on the phone (`mobile/R07-mode`), and playfield labels are unreadable (`mobile/R03-play`).

### Authenticity: 7.0
- **The Row:** all eight bathhouses are labelled in the correct north-to-south order (Superior, Hale, Maurice, Fordyce | Quapaw, Ozark, Buckstaff, Lamar). Quapaw's tiled dome and Fordyce's skylight are recognisable (`desktop/C01-row`, `desktop/C06-arlington`).
- **Landmarks:** the twin-towered Arlington, the lattice Mountain Tower (`desktop/C05-tower`), a Vapors neon sign (`desktop/C04-vapors`), 143°F springs with steam (`desktop/C02-springs`), and the Central Avenue and Reserve St street names.
- **Facts:** the 1832 reservation fact is hedged and correct (`desktop/R09-help`).
- **Weak:** Hale, Maurice, Ozark, Buckstaff and Lamar are near-interchangeable cream boxes. The Arlington sits in the middle of the Row rather than at its north end, across Central.

## IP check
- **Clean.** I found no commercial pinball machine name, logo or art.
- "The Vapors" and the bathhouse names are local landmarks. They are presented as setting, and the "no affiliation" note is in `desktop/R09-help`.
- I saw no text inside the paintings: the backglass, the upper grotto painting (`desktop/C02-springs`) and the terrazzo are free of lettering. All lettering is rendered as separate decals.
- The Vapors sign is a generic neon treatment, not a copy of a real business logo.

## Ranked gaps

1. **G-landscape-framing** (mobile, severity 4, effort M, WS6, gain +0.5 to +1.0): `mobile/R11-landscape` uses about 40% of the screen for the table.
   - **Fix:** in landscape, lower the camera tilt and fit the playfield's projected bounds to the left 70% of the viewport. Pin the HUD column to the remaining 30%.
   - **Test:** the playfield bounding box is at least 60% of the 844 px width, with no black band wider than 40 px.

2. **G-toy-plinths-walls** (toys, severity 4, effort M, WS2, gain +0.5):
   - **Fix:**
     - Replace the cream box plinths under each bathhouse with bevelled stone steps and stoops matched to each facade, or sink the models straight onto the playfield.
     - Give the scoop and lock walls a bevelled stainless or painted-metal profile with a rolled top, like the new ramp blades.
     - Give each bathhouse one signature feature: Buckstaff's columned porch, Ozark's twin towers, Lamar's flat-roof cornice, Hale's portico, Maurice's arched loggia.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/C01-row`, `desktop/C04-vapors`.

3. **G-flipper-sling-rubber** (toys, severity 3, effort S, WS2, gain +0.3):
   - **Fix:**
     - Model a black or red rubber ring around each slingshot and around the flipper tip radius.
     - Add a subtle moulded logo or texture to the flipper top.
     - Add a visible sling kicker arm.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/C03-flippers`.

4. **G-spinner-rim-glare** (lighting, severity 3, effort S, WS1, gain +0.3):
   - **Fix:** the spinner edge still renders a hard white bar, and the lock wall top edge clips. Clamp the specular on the spinner flag edge and the lock wall rims below the bloom threshold, or roughen their bevels.
   - **Test:** no pixel above 0.95 luminance on the spinner or the lock walls in the r03 and c06 captures.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/R03-play`, `mobile/R03-play`, `desktop/C06-arlington`, `desktop/C04-vapors`.

5. **G-desktop-ball-size** (readability, severity 3, effort M, WS6, gain +0.5):
   - **Fix:** the desktop ball is about 12 px. Push the desktop playfield to about 80% of viewport height in width by moving the backglass art into a narrower strip or behind the DMD. Alternatively, add a faint ball rim light or a contact shadow so it pops off the terrazzo.
   - **Test:** ball diameter of at least 16 px at 1280×800.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/R03-play`, `desktop/R06-multiball`.

6. **G-multiball-strip-framing** (animation, severity 3, effort S, WS3, gain +0.3):
   - **Fix:** the S03 capture camera crops off the flippers and shows no launched balls. Frame the strip on the full field and capture ticks where two or three balls are visible leaving the shooter lane and lock.
   - **Also:** add a bumper skirt drop on hit and a short ball trail at high speed.
   - **Evidence:** `strips/S03-multiball`, `strips/S02-bumpers`.

7. **G-apron-and-midfield-art** (art, severity 3, effort M, WS4, gain +0.3):
   - **Fix:**
     - Paint the apron as part of the table, with deco borders and a rule card styled as a bathhouse menu board.
     - Theme the empty terrazzo between the Vapors and the ramp foot, for example with a Central Avenue streetscape inlay.
     - Restyle the four arrow colours as framed deco inserts in one family.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/R02-serve`, `desktop/C04-vapors`.

8. **G-hud-state-truth** (ui, severity 3, effort S, WS5, gain +0.3):
   - **Fix:**
     - Make the mode grid reflect the real state: all six won when the wizard is running.
     - Add a tiny legend, or use lamp-style states (dim, lit, flashing) instead of a gold fill and a purple outline.
     - Show the mode timer and progress on the phone as a second pill next to the score.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/R08-wizard`, `mobile/R07-mode`.

9. **G-menu-deco-type** (typography, severity 2, effort S, WS5, gain +0.3):
   - **Fix:**
     - Load a deliberate body face (a geometric deco sans) instead of the system UI font.
     - Give buttons deco corner frames or chevrons.
     - Fix the help list so the last item isn't cut mid-line behind the Back bar: add bottom padding or a visible scroll affordance.
   - **Evidence:** `desktop/R09-help`, `mobile/R09-help`, `desktop/R01-title`.

10. **G-skill-shot-cue** (readability, severity 2, effort S, WS5, gain +0.2):
    - **Fix:**
      - Flash the target S·P·A lane insert with a halo.
      - Change the DMD to "SKILL SHOT: S·P·A — LANE A".
      - Add a pulsing arrow at the plunger lane exit.
    - **Evidence:** `desktop/R02-serve`, `mobile/R02-serve`.
