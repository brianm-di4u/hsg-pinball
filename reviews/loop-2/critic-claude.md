# Critic A (critic-claude): loop 2

## Verdict

Spa City Pinball now has one continuous playfield painting composed around the layout: a wooded mountain with a tufa cascade up top, the promenade wall behind the Row, and a terrazzo deco fan running down to the flippers. The backglass is painted in the same hand. That is a real step, and the A/B pairs show it clearly over the masked-zone build. Next to Pinball FX or a good VPX table, though, the 3D layer still looks like a prototype. The walls are untextured dark boxes with flat gold caps (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C06-arlington`). The bathhouses are beige blocks the size of fingernails (`desktop/C01-row`). The flippers are plain white slabs, the slingshots have no rubber, the ramp is a set of translucent planes, and the "wireform" reads as glowing neon tubes (`desktop/C05-tower`). Lighting barely changes between plain play, multiball, a mode and the wizard mode (`desktop/R03-play` vs `R06-multiball`, `R07-mode`, `R08-wizard`), so the promised mood shifts are not visible. The DMD's "images" are dithered noise. On the phone the DMD shrank this loop to make room for a progress line (`ab/mobile-r07-mode`). Art and authenticity are near their gates. Toys, lighting and animation are the furthest away.

## Scores

### Art: 6.5
- `desktop/R03-play`, `desktop/R02-serve`: the playfield is now one painting whose zones follow the geometry. The forest and cascade sit under the bumpers, the stone promenade sits behind the target banks, and the terrazzo fan's rays point into the inlanes. The backglass (magnolias, domed bathhouse, lattice tower, wet neon street) is clearly by the same hand.
- `desktop/C02-springs`, `desktop/C01-row`: the painting quality holds up close: spring house, cascade, flowers, brick plaza.
- Against: the lower third is a large, mostly even terrazzo field. Its only features are four 2X–5X inserts and a star, so the art does not lead the eye up to the shots (`desktop/C03-flippers`, `mobile/R03-play`).
- Lettering collisions: the painted "VAPORS" label is cut off by the SPA CITY logo, so only "RS" shows (`desktop/C04-vapors`). The arrow insert sits on top of "MOUNTAIN TOWER" (`mobile/R02-serve`, `mobile/R11-landscape`).
- At desktop scale the painting reads as busy foliage noise behind the upper shots (`desktop/R03-play`).

### Toys: 5.0
- `desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C06-arlington`: the walls and guides are extruded rectangles in a flat dark material with flat ochre caps. They have no bevel, no wood grain or lacquer, and no screws. Post rubbers are white half-discs. This is the rubric's "rails and walls read as blocks" anchor.
- `desktop/C01-row`: the eight bathhouses are tiny flat-shaded blocks with window decals. Only the Quapaw dome and the twin-towered Buckstaff/Fordyce shapes are distinct. Unlit target inserts are flat grey-brown rectangles.
- `desktop/C03-flippers`: the flippers are featureless white slabs with a red stripe, with no decal, bevel or bat-shaped taper. The slingshots are coloured plastic triangles on white blocks, with no visible rubber.
- `desktop/C05-tower`: the ramp is translucent planes with a visible seam and no flap or entrance lip detail. The wire habitrail renders as emissive blue-white lines rather than chrome wire. The Mountain Tower lattice model is the best toy on the table.
- `desktop/C02-springs`: the bumper caps are clean and the decal now reads upright, but the bodies are a matte peach cylinder that looks like skin, not lit plastic. The S·P·A lane guides are flat gold slabs.
- The Vapors toy is a small white box with a tiny vertical neon sign that is unreadable at playfield scale (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/R03-play`).

### Lighting: 5.5
- `desktop/C03-flippers`, `desktop/C01-row`: lit inserts bloom correctly and only where lit, and warm GI now lifts the lower field.
- `desktop/C06-arlington`: a large blue-white glare blob hangs over the left playfield. A hot white rollover lamp blows out at top left in `desktop/C04-vapors`. The rubric's 7 requires "no glare".
- Toys have almost no contact shadows. The bathhouse blocks and the Arlington model sit on the art without grounding (`desktop/C01-row`, `desktop/C06-arlington`).
- Mood: `desktop/R06-multiball`, `R07-mode` and `R08-wizard` look nearly identical to `R03-play` in overall key and fill. The wizard only adds lit inserts (the mode arc, bathhouse row, all four multipliers), with no visible dimming or colour shift. `strips/S03-multiball` shows an arc chase, but the room around it stays the same.
- The ball carries a chrome reflection with a horizon band (`desktop/C03-flippers`, `mobile/R11-landscape`), which is good.

### Animation: 5.5
- `strips/S01-flip`: the left flipper snaps up between frames 2 and 3 and holds. The ball travels cleanly upfield. There is no ball trail and no lamp reaction.
- `strips/S02-bumpers`: bumper caps and skirts flash peach-bright (frames 0–4) and decay by frame 5, and a slight reframe suggests the camera kick. No bumper ring drop or skirt movement is readable.
- `strips/S03-multiball`: the mode arc lights in a purple chase and the bathhouse inserts flash. Steam particles drift. The camera pans to follow the ball.
- No slingshot kick or flash appears in any strip, and there is no anticipation or follow-through on events. The steam reads as soft grey blobs (`desktop/C02-springs`).

### UI: 6.0
- `desktop/R02-serve`, `desktop/R03-play`: the amber dot-matrix DMD in a gold frame has some character. The side panel (ball, score, Modes/Tower progress, key chips, Menu/Launch) is clear. The menus use one gold-and-navy style (`desktop/R01-title`, `desktop/R10-scores`).
- `desktop/R07-mode`, `desktop/R08-wizard`: the DMD's "art" panel is dithered noise with no readable image. The text is crammed into four lines ("• 60 SECONDS"), and the DMD does not tell the mode's story.
- `desktop/R09-help`: the last bullet (The Vapors) is ghosted half-under the pinned Back bar, with no scroll affordance.
- The desktop side panel is half empty below the buttons (`desktop/R03-play`). On mobile the score shows twice, in the DMD and the chip (`mobile/R03-play`).

### Typography: 6.0
- `desktop/R01-title`, `mobile/R01-title`: the glowing slab-serif "PINBALL" lockup is deliberate and handsome. The menu body face is a generic system sans.
- `desktop/C01-row`: the bathhouse labels now fit their slots ("MAURICE", "BUCKSTAFF", "LAMAR"). "HALE" is still cut off by the left kicker housing.
- At full-table scale, insert lettering (SOAK, DERBY, RAID, 2X–5X, SHOOT AGAIN) is a few pixels high and illegible on desktop (`desktop/R03-play`). The apron cards are barely legible on the phone (`mobile/R02-serve`).
- The mobile DMD's four lines of 5×7 type are cramped (`mobile/R07-mode`, `mobile/R08-wizard`).

### Readability: 6.0
- `desktop/R06-multiball`, `mobile/R06-multiball`: all three chrome balls are findable against the warm terrazzo. `desktop/R05-ramp` and `mobile/R05-ramp`: the ball on the clear ramp is visible but small.
- `desktop/R04-bumpers`, `mobile/R04-bumpers`: the ball is in the upper arch above the bumpers, and over the busy foliage it is the weakest read.
- Lit and unlit purple mode inserts are distinct. The lit S·P·A skill-shot lane in `desktop/R02-serve` is not obvious, since the lit and unlit lanes barely differ.
- On desktop the table is only about 430 px wide of 1280 (`desktop/R03-play`), which shrinks every shot cue.

### Mobile: 6.0
- `mobile/R03-play`, `mobile/R06-multiball`: portrait fills the width on purpose, and the Launch button sits over the apron, never the ball's area.
- `mobile/R11-landscape`: landscape now fits the width and follows the ball. It only shows the bottom half (flippers through the mode arc), so the bumpers, Row and ramp entrance are off-screen. The side panel is mostly empty.
- `mobile/R07-mode`, `mobile/R08-wizard`: the DMD shrank to about 190 css px to make room for the progress text, and four lines of dot type are marginal. The score is shown twice.

### Authenticity: 6.5
- `desktop/C01-row`, `desktop/R09-help`: all eight bathhouses are named in the correct order (Superior, Hale, Maurice, Fordyce, Quapaw, Ozark, Buckstaff, Lamar). The Quapaw model has a mosaic dome.
- `desktop/C06-arlington`, `desktop/C05-tower`: the twin-towered Arlington and the lattice Mountain Tower are recognisable. The tufa cascade and spring box (`desktop/C02-springs`) and the magnolia and crepe-myrtle backglass (`desktop/R03-play`) read as local. The help text hedges its facts.
- Most bathhouse models are interchangeable beige blocks, and the Vapors is a generic white box (`desktop/C04-vapors`). A local would name the Arlington and the Tower, not most of the Row.

## IP check
No commercial pinball machine names, logos or trade dress seen. The game title, "Spa City", "Valley of the Vapors" and the Hot Springs place names are generic or geographic. I saw no lettering inside the playfield or backglass paintings: all playfield text is vector overlay, and the backglass neon at right has no legible words. Clean.

4. **G-bathhouse-models** (authenticity, WS2, sev 4, L, +0.75): model each bathhouse's signature feature (Fordyce's stained-glass canopy, Buckstaff's columns, Maurice's arched portico, Ozark's twin towers) at about twice the current height, with contact shadows. Test: each of the eight is distinguishable in `desktop/C01-row` without its label.
5. **G-dmd-art** (ui, WS5, sev 4, M, +0.75): replace the dithered noise panels with real 1-bit mode animations (horse race, tower climb, steam) and limit each screen to two lines of text. Test: `desktop/R07-mode` shows a recognisable picture and at most two text lines.
6. **G-mobile-dmd** (mobile, WS6, sev 3, S, +0.5): restore the full-width mobile DMD and move the progress text into the score chip row, so the score is not duplicated. Test: in `mobile/R07-mode` the DMD is at least 300 css px wide.
7. **G-ramp-wireform** (toys, WS2, sev 3, M, +0.5): render the habitrail as metallic tubes with environment reflections, not emissive lines, and give the ramp an entrance flap and side rails with thickness. Test: in `desktop/C05-tower` the wires show a specular highlight and a shadow.
8. **G-flipper-model** (toys, WS2, sev 2, S, +0.25): taper the flipper bats, add a top decal and a rubber with thickness, and give the bumper bodies a translucent plastic look. Test: `desktop/C03-flippers` and `desktop/C02-springs`.
9. **G-label-collisions** (art, WS4, sev 2, S, +0.25): move the "VAPORS" painted label clear of the SPA CITY logo, keep the arrow insert off "MOUNTAIN TOWER", and keep the HALE label clear of the kicker. Test: `desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C01-row`, `mobile/R02-serve`.
10. **G-desktop-framing** (readability, WS6, sev 3, M, +0.5): give the table more of the desktop width (shrink the backglass or tilt the camera) and make the lit skill-shot lane flash strongly. Test: the table is at least 520 px wide in `desktop/R03-play`, and the lit lane is obvious in `R02-serve`.
11. **G-lower-field** (art, WS4, sev 2, M, +0.25): add painted lane-direction cues or shot-coloured rays to the terrazzo fan that point at the lit shots. Test: `desktop/C03-flippers`.
12. **G-sling-anim** (animation, WS3, sev 3, S, +0.5): capture and strengthen the slingshot kick (rubber stretch, plastic flash) and add a short ball trail on fast shots. Test: a strip frame shows the sling flash.
