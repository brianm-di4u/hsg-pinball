# Critic A (critic-claude): loop 1

## Verdict

Spa City Pinball is a handsome browser table that still reads as a well-made hobby build rather than a shipped Zen or Pro Pinball table. The upper third is the strongest part: the painted thermal-springs art, the dot-matrix DMD and the neon backglass painting are in one confident hand. Below the Arlington the table goes thin. The lower playfield is a dark navy field with a painted fan pasted on as a rectangle, and horizontal seams show where the art panels meet (`desktop/C03-flippers` y≈215, `desktop/C01-row` y≈285 and y≈600, `desktop/s02-bumpers-1` y≈630). The toys are recognisable low-poly blocks in flat materials. The ramp is a translucent box with a kink at its entrance and no real wireform. The slingshots show no rubber, and post rubbers render as two white crescents. The bumper caps read "143°F" upside down. The playfield labels on the new plates are now truncated ("MAURIC", "BUCKSTAF", with "OZARK" overlapped). The menu hint line is clipped behind the Settings button on both viewports. Wizard mode, which the DMD says has "all shots lit", looks almost the same as normal play. Animation and lighting reach the "lamps light and things react" level but not the "lit machine in a dark room" level. Every gated category is below its gate, and authenticity only just clears its gate.

## Scores

### Art: 5.5
- The painted upper playfield (travertine pools, stairs, lanterns) is rich, and the backglass painting (`desktop/R03-play` left panel) matches it in hand. The new apron with enamel, gold trim and two cards (`desktop/R03-play`, `mobile/R03-play`) finally closes off the bottom.
- The art does not cover the layout. In `desktop/C03-flippers` the painted fan ends at a hard straight edge at y≈215, and everything below it is flat dark navy with faint rays. In `desktop/C01-row` there are visible brightness seams across the whole field at y≈285 (above the Arlington scoop) and y≈600 (below LOCK). In `desktop/s02-bumpers-1` the waterfall painting stops on a straight line into the hex-tile floor at y≈630. This is the rubric's "art sits in rectangles unrelated to the layout".
- The lower half of the playfield, between the slings and around the mode arc (`desktop/R03-play`, `mobile/R03-play`), is mostly empty dark space with small labels. Zen tables paint right down to the flipper return lanes.
- Layout collision: the "THE VAPORS" lane label is half hidden under the SPA CITY logo, with only "RS" showing (`desktop/C04-vapors`).

### Toys: 5.5
- What's good: the Mountain Tower now holds up in close-up, with lattice legs, an octagonal lit cab and an antenna (`desktop/C05-tower`). The Arlington's twin towers and red roofs read as a building (`desktop/C06-arlington`). Quapaw's tiled dome and Ozark's twin white towers can be told apart (`desktop/C06-arlington`, `desktop/C01-row`).
- What's not: the bathhouses are box-plus-roof blocks with painted window grids, and at play distance they read as cream rectangles (`desktop/R03-play`). The ramp is flat translucent panels with blocky side walls and a visible kink at y≈520 (`desktop/C05-tower`). There is no metal wireform, and the glowing blue-white tubes along the right rail look like neon strips, not wire. The flippers are plain white extrusions with a red stripe and no bevel or logo (`desktop/C03-flippers`). The slingshots are plastic triangles on a white block with no visible rubber band (`desktop/C03-flippers`). Post rubbers render as two white crescents ("eyes") instead of rings (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C06-arlington`). The bumper caps are flat teal discs with the "143°F" decal upside down to the player (`desktop/C02-springs`, `desktop/s02-bumpers-1`), and the bodies are matte peach with no translucency. The S·P·A lane guides are plain gold bars (`desktop/C02-springs`). The ball is glassy rather than chrome: it shows the art through itself (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`).

### Lighting: 6.0
- Lit inserts glow, and the bloom stays where it belongs. The 2X/3X versus 4X/5X contrast is clean (`desktop/C03-flippers`). The rail glare streak is gone and the halos are tighter (`ab/desktop-c02-springs`). The multiball strip shows a real light show: the Row inserts and the purple mode arc chase on and off across the frames (`strips/S03-multiball`, `desktop/s03-multiball-5`).
- The rest of the field is lit flatly. There are no GI pools under the slings or the arch, and the lower playfield is just dark (`desktop/R03-play`). The contact shadows are barely visible: there is a faint smudge under the flippers in `ab/desktop-c03-flippers` B and almost nothing under the bathhouses (`desktop/C01-row`). Wizard mode (`desktop/R08-wizard`, `mobile/R08-wizard`) looks almost the same as normal play (`desktop/R03-play`): the mode arc is unlit and nothing changes in colour or mood, so the "light show" frame does not show one. The ball's reflection reads, but in the painted top it washes into the waterfalls (`desktop/R04-bumpers`).

### Animation: 6.0
- `strips/S01-flip`: the left flipper snaps up between frames 2 and 3 and holds, and the ball leaves cleanly. There is no anticipation, no smear and no rebound on the flipper.
- `strips/S02-bumpers`: the bumper bodies flash orange on contact (frames 0–4) and fade out over 5–7, and the steam puffs drift. There is no ring or skirt movement and no lamp flash on the playfield around the bumper.
- `strips/S03-multiball`: the inserts chase in sequence and the camera shifts sideways across the frames, so the table reacts. This is the best evidence for 7. But the flipper and bumper events have no follow-through, and no ball trail, sparks or score pop-ups show in any strip.

### UI: 5.5
- The DMD has character, with an orange dot matrix in a brass bezel (`desktop/R02-serve`). But the new four-line layouts are crammed edge to edge ("ARLINGTON / MULTIBALL / RAMP FOR / JACKPOTS", `desktop/R06-multiball`, `mobile/R06-multiball`). The "dithered mode paintings" read as orange noise with no subject you can make out (`desktop/R07-mode`, `mobile/R07-mode`).
- The menus are generic purple rounded rectangles with a gold primary button, not the deco style of the cabinet (`desktop/R01-title`, `desktop/R10-scores`). The hint line under Settings is clipped through the middle of its glyphs on both viewports (`desktop/R01-title` "Shift or Z and / to flip", `mobile/R01-title` "Tap the left or right side to flip"). In help, the last bullet sits half hidden behind the pinned Back button with no fade (`desktop/R09-help`, `mobile/R09-help`). On phones the score is shown twice (`mobile/R03-play`: DMD plus plate).

### Typography: 5.0
- The display type is good: the "SPA CITY / PINBALL" neon slab serif and the "How to play" heading have character (`desktop/R01-title`, `desktop/R09-help`). The DMD font is clean.
- The playfield lettering is broken in close-up. The new plates truncate the names: "MAURIC", "BUCKSTAF", "OZARK" overlapped by its neighbour, and "HALE" partly behind a post (`desktop/C01-row`, `desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C05-tower`). "THE VAPORS" is under the logo (`desktop/C04-vapors`). The bumper decal is upside down (`desktop/C02-springs`). At 390 px the playfield labels are 4–5 css px and can't be read (`mobile/R03-play`). The menu hint text is clipped (`mobile/R01-title`). The UI body copy is a stock system sans with no relation to the display face (`desktop/R10-scores`).

### Readability: 5.5
- The ball reads well on the dark lower field as a bright glint (`desktop/R03-play`, `mobile/R03-play`). The three multiball balls are all findable (`desktop/R06-multiball`).
- It is lost in the painted upper third. In `desktop/R04-bumpers` the ball near the S lane at the top is a pale speck against white water. The shot lamps are small. Wizard "all shots lit" leaves the mode arc dark (`desktop/R08-wizard`), so the lamps contradict the DMD. The skill-shot lane lamp in `desktop/R02-serve` is a single small dot. Shot entrances such as the Vapors scoop and the orbit are only told apart by small arrows.

### Mobile: 6.0
- Portrait fills the width with the table and a readable DMD. The score plate is a sensible addition. Launch now sits over the apron, clear of the shooter lane (`mobile/R02-serve`), but it covers the right instruction card, whose text shows through the button.
- In landscape the table occupies only about 700 of 1280 px. There is roughly 260 px of black on the left and more on the right, and the side panel is two-thirds empty (`mobile/R11-landscape`). The steam particles float in the void above the table. The playfield text can't be read in portrait. The title hint is clipped (`mobile/R01-title`).

### Authenticity: 6.5
- The eight bathhouses are named in the correct north–south order: Superior, Hale, Maurice, Fordyce, Quapaw, Ozark, Buckstaff, Lamar (`desktop/C01-row`, `mobile/R09-help`). The Arlington has twin towers (`desktop/C06-arlington`), the Mountain Tower has its lattice (`desktop/C05-tower`), and the Vapors scoop is there (`desktop/C04-vapors`). There are Central Ave and Reserve St labels, 143 °F, rising steam, and hedged facts in help.
- Against that: the springs painting shows turquoise travertine terraces in the style of Pamukkale (`desktop/C02-springs`). They are pretty, but they are not what Hot Springs' tufa cascades and open springs look like. The Vapors sign is a tiny vertical neon strip you can't read at play distance (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/R03-play`). Apart from Quapaw and Ozark, the bathhouses are interchangeable cream boxes, so a local could not name Fordyce or Buckstaff from the models.

## IP check

Clean on what is visible. No commercial pinball machine's name, title, logo or art appears. The playfield painting and the backglass painting contain no legible text. The neon shapes in the backglass carry no lettering. The DMD dither paintings are abstract noise. The real names that do appear are places (bathhouses, streets, the Arlington), presented as setting, with a no-affiliation note in help (`desktop/R09-help`).
