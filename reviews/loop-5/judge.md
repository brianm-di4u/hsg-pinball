# Judge: loop 5

The reviewers disagree very little. The critics differ by at most 0.5 in every category, so neither total moves far from 59.5. The prosecutor and advocate are at most 1.0 apart (art, toys and animation). No category crossed the brief's ">1.0" trigger, but several factual claims conflict, so I opened the close-ups, the strips and the relevant frames and checked each one. Each ruling below says who was right.

**My total is 60.0 / 90.** UI and authenticity meet their gates. Every other category sits at 6.5, so the verdict is **continue**. This loop's big lever, rebuilding the landmarks, clearly paid off in the close-ups (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c06-arlington`). The headline lighting fix did not reach the stills.

## Factual disputes I settled by opening the images

- **Do lit bathhouse plates erase the names? (Critic A says yes, the advocate says no.)** The advocate is right. In `desktop/c01-row` the lit rectangles are lamp inserts that sit above separate dark name tags, and FORDYCE and OZARK both read cleanly under the brightest lamps. A's G-plate-glare has the wrong cause, so I dropped it. Real glare does exist elsewhere: a white bar across the CENTRAL AVENUE plate and the washed-out Vapors marquee (`desktop/c04-vapors`), and an edge-on white streak at the left orbit (`desktop/r03-play`, about x 462–492, y 270).
- **Is the ramp an opaque slab, or does it have seams? (A says slab, the advocate says it transmits, the prosecutor says seams.)** Each is partly right, and the prosecutor's finding matters most. In `desktop/c05-tower` the playfield brick does show through the ramp bed, so it is not opaque, and A is wrong there. But I see two transverse seams, one at the bend and one about two-thirds down, and the left wall steps inward at the lower seam. The "highlights" are a regular white zipper-tooth pattern, not a continuous flange edge. The ramp is visibly built from separate pieces.
- **What are the slings made of? (A says flat colour fills, the advocate says translucent plastic, the prosecutor says boxes.)** In `desktop/c03-flippers` the plastics are translucent and have a sheen, so they are not flat fills, and the advocate is right on that. They carry no artwork. The sling bodies are white blocks with two black holes, with no rubber band and no posts. The prosecutor is right that this is a primitive assembly.
- **Does anything on the bumpers move? (A says nothing, Codex and the advocate say the caps move.)** Codex and the advocate are right, but the movement is small. I cropped the left bumper across `desktop/s02-bumpers-0…7`: the glowing body band is short in frames 0–1 and clearly taller in frames 2–3. Nobody would call it a kick at play scale.
- **Mood darkening (Codex says it "helps"; A, the prosecutor and the advocate measured almost none).** Codex is wrong. I sampled median luma on unlit patches in the desktop frames:

  | Patch | r03 | r07 | r06 | r08 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Lower left | 80 | 77 | 73 | 70 |
  | Lower right | 76 | 74 | 72 | 70 |
  | Garden | 63 | 59 | 55 | 56 |

  - Against normal play that is about 0.95 in the mode frame, 0.90 in multiball and 0.88 in the wizard frame.
  - The Grand Soak strip *does* dim. In `desktop/s03-multiball-1…6` an upper-left patch falls from 59 to 45 (about 0.76).
  - So the mood scalar exists, but it is shallow, and the darkening is inconsistent between multiballs, as the prosecutor said.
- **Arlington position (only the prosecutor raised it).** Confirmed. In `desktop/c06-arlington` and `desktop/c01-row` the Arlington sits directly on the gold lock-gate crossbar, with no ground and no shadow.
- **Ball visibility (Codex says it is lost on the ramp; the advocate disagrees).** It depends on the viewport.
  - Phone: the advocate is right. In `mobile/r05-ramp` the ball on the ramp (about 655, 655) is bright and warm.
  - Desktop: the prosecutor is right. In `desktop/r05-ramp` the ball is a faint pale dot at about 785, 237.
  - `desktop/r04-bumpers` shows the ball at the top arch, not in the bumpers, with every cap idle, so the capture does not show what it is named for.
  - In `strips/s02-bumpers` frames 0–2 the ball reads as a glowing peach orb rather than chrome, as A and the prosecutor said. In `desktop/c03-flippers` it is clean chrome.
- **Wizard inserts as one "violet band" (Codex says they merge).** Mostly wrong. In `mobile/r08-wizard` and `desktop/r08-wizard` the six discs stay separate and their labels sit under them. The star's bloom does crowd the VALLEY OF THE VAPORS label.
- **Help text size and layout (A says about 11 px; the prosecutor says columns are unbalanced).** In `desktop/r09-help` the body type is about 15 px, so A is wrong about the size. The prosecutor is right about the layout: the left column is cut mid-sentence under the fade ("Central Avenue orbit … Fifth Season meter for an extra ball"), while the right column ends at about y 480 with roughly 220 px of empty panel.
- **Phone steam.** It is still small bubble dots (`mobile/r08-wizard`, `mobile/r11-landscape`). In landscape the dots float in the black above the table.
