# Critic A (critic-claude): loop 5

## Verdict

Spa City Pinball is now a coherent, attractive table that clearly knows its subject. The backglass painting is very good, the Row is in the correct order with readable signature features in the close-ups, the DMD has picture icons with character, and the portrait phone layout is deliberate. Next to a shipped Zen or Pro Pinball table it still falls short in four places, and they show in every frame. (1) The materials on the big pieces are not convincing yet. The Mountain Tower ramp is a matte grey slab, the lane guides are fat brass pipes, and in the bumpers the ball reads as a peach glass marble rather than chrome. (2) The headline lighting change, mood darkening, is close to invisible in the stills. On unlit art patches the mode frame is no darker than normal play, and the wizard frame is only about 10% darker, not the stated ~35% of normal light. (3) Playfield micro-type is illegible at play scale on both viewports. (4) Desktop and landscape framing waste the screen. The loop's changes are visible and mostly positive: the new Vapors supper-club marquee, more detailed bathhouses, a gold focus ring, Launch hidden until needed, softer steam, every insert lit in the wizard show. But none of them lifts a category clearly past its gate. Build A is this loop's improvement in almost every pair.

## Scores

### Art: 6.5
- The backglass in `desktop/R02-serve` and `desktop/R08-wizard` is the strongest asset in the game. It is a painterly night scene of a domed bathhouse, a lattice tower on the mountain, a neon supper-club canopy, wet-street reflections and magnolias, and it reads as the same hand and palette as the upper playfield.
- The upper playfield (`desktop/C02-springs`, `strips/S02-bumpers`) is a dense painted grotto with a waterfall, spring pool and stone arch. It is lush but generic. It could be any garden, and it does not point toward the shots. Its busy detail fights the bumpers and the ball.
- The lower half (`desktop/C03-flippers`, `desktop/R03-play`) is a deco fan-and-terrazzo pattern that frames the flippers sensibly. It is plain next to the top half, and the flat coral and teal sling plastics read as colour fills, not artwork.
- The inserts sit on plates and are integrated with the layout (`desktop/R03-play`: the SOAK…COASTER arc and the 2X–5X row). But the field is still two pictures, a garden and a floor, rather than one composition designed around the shots.
- This is short of the 7 anchor, "every square inch designed".

### Toys: 6.5
- The bathhouses in `desktop/C01-row` and `desktop/C06-arlington` now have cornices, lit windows and individual silhouettes: Quapaw's tiled dome with lantern, Ozark's twin towers, Hale's red hip roof, the white neoclassical Buckstaff. The Arlington's twin turrets and red roofs read instantly in `desktop/C06-arlington`.
- But they still sit on identical plain cream plinth boxes, and they show no wear or weathering. At play scale (`desktop/R03-play`) they shrink to a row of cream blocks.
- The new Vapors in `desktop/C04-vapors` is a real improvement, with a canopy and a neon-edged marquee. But "The VAPORS" lettering is pale cyan on cyan bloom and barely legible even in the close-up.
- The ramp in `desktop/C05-tower` and `desktop/R08-wizard` is the weakest toy. It is a flat, matte, light-grey slab with brown side walls. There is no clear-plastic transmission, no edge highlights and no decal, so it reads like a grey board. This is the anchor-5 failure, "rails and walls read as grey blocks".
- The wireform and the lattice tower with its octagonal lantern (`desktop/C05-tower`) are convincing.
- The bumpers (`desktop/C02-springs`) are good: teal caps with the 143 °F decal, chrome skirts.
- The flippers (`desktop/C03-flippers`) are clean white with red rubber but carry no detail.
- The lane guides around the Vapors and the Arlington (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C06-arlington`) are oversized brass cylinders that read like plumbing.
- The ball looks chrome in `strips/S01-flip`. In `strips/S02-bumpers` frames 0–2 it turns into a translucent peach marble.

### Lighting: 6.5
- Inserts bloom only when lit, and lit and unlit inserts differ clearly (`desktop/R03-play`: SOAK lit, the other mode inserts dull).
- The GI is warm, and the bumper skirts glow teal (`desktop/C02-springs`).
- The mood lighting claimed this loop does not show. I sampled median luma on unlit art patches in the phone frames. In `mobile/R03-play` they read 75 / 77 / 76. In `mobile/R07-mode` they read 74 / 78 / 77, so the mode frame is not darker at all. In `mobile/R08-wizard` they read 66 / 71 / 76, roughly 10% darker against a stated ~35% of normal light. By eye, `desktop/R08-wizard` and `desktop/R07-mode` look like ordinary play with more lamps on, not like a machine in a dark room.
- The wizard light show itself works. All six mode inserts, the star and every shot arrow are lit in `desktop/R08-wizard` and `mobile/R08-wizard`.
- There is glare. Lit building plates blow out to featureless white rectangles in `desktop/C01-row` (FORDYCE, OZARK) and `desktop/C04-vapors`, and the plate text vanishes.
- Contact shadows under the buildings and posts are weak (`desktop/C06-arlington`).
- The wire rails no longer streak white (`desktop/C05-tower`).

### Animation: 6.5
- `strips/S01-flip`: the left flipper is down in frames 0–2 and fully up in frame 3, and the ball leaves cleanly up the field through frames 4–7. The snap reads, but there is no anticipation, no rubber flex and no smear.
- `strips/S02-bumpers`: the ball ricochets between the three caps, and the caps and skirts flash on and off from frame to frame (right bumper lit in frames 0–1, dark in frames 4–6). There is no visible ring or skirt kick. Large soft steam puffs drift over the springs, an improvement on dots.
- `strips/S03-multiball`: a real light show. The insert arc pulses purple in frames 4–6, the lane and arrow lamps chase, and the balls feed from the shooter lane and the top. But the camera never reacts and the GI never shifts.
- On phone the steam still renders as small bubble dots (`mobile/R08-wizard` and `mobile/R11-landscape`, above the bumpers).

### UI: 7.0
- The DMD has character. There are dot-matrix icons for Arlington Multiball (towers), Derby Day (horse and rider) and Valley of the Vapors (steaming mountain) in `desktop/R06-multiball`, `desktop/R07-mode` and `desktop/R08-wizard`, plus a clear two-line layout.
- The HUD chip "Modes 2/6 · Tower 1/3" (`mobile/R03-play`) is clean.
- The menus (`desktop/R01-title`, `mobile/R09-help`, `mobile/R10-scores`) share the gold-framed deco panel, and the gold focus ring now matches the style.
- Desktop Launch is hidden when no ball is in the lane (`desktop/R03-play` against `desktop/R02-serve`).
- Weaknesses:
  - On desktop the right column is a small DMD floating in a lot of empty navy (`desktop/R08-wizard`).
  - The keyboard hints are tiny.
  - Phone steam drifts up out of the table into the backbox region (`mobile/R03-play`).

### Typography: 6.5
- The deliberate faces work. There is a neon serif "PINBALL" lockup with tracked "SPA CITY", a slab-serif "How to play" and "High scores", and a clean amber DMD dot font (`desktop/R01-title`, `mobile/R09-help`, `mobile/R08-wizard`).
- The body and menu text is a stock system sans that adds no character (`mobile/R09-help`, `desktop/R09-help`).
- Playfield lettering is crisp in the close-ups (`desktop/C03-flippers` SHOOT AGAIN, `desktop/C06-arlington` THE ARLINGTON). At play scale it is illegible: "BATHHOUSE ROW · HOT SPRINGS", "VALLEY OF THE VAPORS", the green "EXTRA BALL" (which is mush) and the insert labels in `desktop/R03-play` are about 5 px. They are worse on `mobile/R03-play`.
- The SUPERIOR plate is still hidden behind the Vapors marquee. `desktop/C01-row` reads "SU… HALE".
- The help text on desktop (`desktop/R09-help`) is a dense two-column block at about 11 px.

### Readability: 6.5
- The ball mostly stands out against the warm field (`desktop/R03-play`, `mobile/R03-play`).
- It is small on desktop because the table fills only about x 410–870 of 1280 (`desktop/R08-wizard`).
- In the busy garden painting near the bumpers it loses contrast (`strips/S02-bumpers`).
- Shot arrows in distinct colours mark the entrances, and lit and unlit inserts are distinct.
- In multiball (`mobile/R06-multiball`) the three balls are findable but not instant. One sits at the top arch over dark painting.
- The skill-shot lane stays lit in `desktop/R02-serve` and `mobile/R02-serve`.
- Blown-out lit plates (`desktop/C01-row`) erase the bathhouse names exactly when they matter.

### Mobile: 6.5
- Portrait is designed on purpose (`mobile/R03-play`, `mobile/R08-wizard`). The DMD sits on top and is readable, the table fills the width, and the Menu and Launch buttons sit in the apron below the flippers, clear of the ball's area.
- Landscape (`mobile/R11-landscape`) is weak:
  - The steep camera leaves a squat trapezoid playfield, with roughly the top 20% of the viewport empty black above the table.
  - The HUD column on the right is mostly empty below the Menu button.
  - The ball and inserts are small.
- On phone the playfield type is unreadable (`mobile/R03-play`), and the Vapors marquee is a tiny white box (`mobile/R08-wizard`).

### Authenticity: 7.0
- The eight bathhouses are in the correct order, Superior → Lamar (`desktop/C01-row`), and each has a recognisable signature: Quapaw dome, Ozark twin towers, Buckstaff's white classical front, Hale's red hip roof.
- The Arlington's twin towers (`desktop/C06-arlington`), the lattice Mountain Tower (`desktop/C05-tower`), the Vapors as a neon supper club (`desktop/C04-vapors`), the steam over the springs, 143 °F, Central Avenue, Reserve St, Derby Day and RAID! 1967 all ground it.
- The help text hedges its facts ("about", "reportedly") in `desktop/R09-help`, and the stray source note is gone.
- The weaknesses:
  - At play scale the Row collapses into near-identical cream blocks.
  - The marquee lettering that should say "The Vapors" is washed out.
  - The garden painting at the top is not specifically Hot Springs, where something like the Grand Promenade or Arlington Lawn would be.

## IP check

- I found nothing resembling a commercial pinball machine's name, logo or art.
- The title, DMD, insert and plate lettering are all overlays.
- I saw no text in the backglass painting or the playfield paintings (`desktop/R02-serve`, `desktop/C02-springs`). The neon canopy in the backglass carries no lettering.
- Low-risk notes:
  - "The Arlington" and "The Vapors" are names of real current or historic venues. They are used as place names in original styling with no logos, and the help text carries a no-affiliation note.
  - Keep the Vapors marquee lettering original. Do not copy the historic sign's script.
- Clean.

## Ranked gaps

1. **G-mood-dim-invisible** (lighting, WS1, severity 4, effort S, expected gain +0.5)
   - Problem: the mode and wizard darkening barely registers (`mobile/R07-mode` and `mobile/R08-wizard` against `mobile/R03-play`).
   - Fix: apply the mood multiplier to the playfield and toy materials' ambient, hemisphere and environment-map intensity, not only to the GI lamp pass.
   - Test: median luma of an unlit art patch in R08 is at most 45% of the same patch in R03, and R07 is at most 80%.
2. **G-ramp-material** (toys, WS2, severity 4, effort M, expected gain +0.5)
   - Problem: the Mountain Tower ramp is a matte grey slab (`desktop/C05-tower`, `desktop/R08-wizard`).
   - Fix: build a clear-plastic material with transmission or fresnel, bright edge highlights on the flanges and side walls, a printed decal or arrow, and the playfield visible through it.
   - Test: in C05 the art under the ramp shows through, and both edges show a highlight line.
3. **G-plate-glare** (lighting, WS1, severity 3, effort S, expected gain +0.3)
   - Problem: lit bathhouse plates blow out to white rectangles and lose their names (`desktop/C01-row`, `desktop/C04-vapors`).
   - Fix: cap insert emissive below the bloom clip, tint it warm amber, and draw the name dark-on-lit.
   - Test: FORDYCE and OZARK are legible while lit in C01.
4. **G-playfield-micro-type** (typography, WS4, severity 3, effort M, expected gain +0.4)
   - Problem: secondary playfield labels are illegible at play scale (`desktop/R03-play`, `mobile/R03-play`).
   - Fix: set a minimum cap height of 7 CSS px at 390 px portrait for insert and plate labels. Drop or merge labels that cannot meet it, such as the "BATHHOUSE ROW · HOT SPRINGS" subline and "EXTRA BALL", and use a darker plate behind green text.
   - Test: every insert label reads in `mobile/R03-play` at 1×.
5. **G-vapors-marquee-legibility** (authenticity, WS2, severity 3, effort S, expected gain +0.3)
   - Problem: "The VAPORS" is pale on its own bloom (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C01-row`).
   - Fix: put a dark marquee backing behind it, thin bright neon tubes with a capped emissive, and a contrasting colour for "The".
   - Test: the word reads in C04, and the marquee reads as a lit sign, not a white box, in `mobile/R03-play`.
6. **G-desktop-table-scale** (readability, WS6, severity 3, effort M, expected gain +0.4)
   - Problem: the table fills about 36% of the 1280 width (`desktop/R08-wizard`), so the ball and inserts are small.
   - Fix: tighten the camera so the table fills the full height at a larger scale, and narrow the backglass and HUD columns.
   - Test: the playfield is at least 45% of the viewport width.
7. **G-landscape-framing** (mobile, WS6, severity 3, effort M, expected gain +0.4)
   - Problem: `mobile/R11-landscape` has a squat steep view with empty black above the table and an empty HUD column.
   - Fix: flatten the camera pitch to fill the height and move the score into a slim side rail.
   - Test: the table spans at least 90% of the viewport height.
8. **G-ball-chrome** (toys, WS1, severity 3, effort S, expected gain +0.3)
   - Problem: the ball reads as peach glass near the bumpers (`strips/S02-bumpers`, frames 0–2).
   - Fix: metalness 1, low roughness, a cooler environment map with a dark horizon so it reflects rather than tints.
   - Test: the ball stays neutral-silver with one sharp highlight in all eight S02 frames.
9. **G-bumper-kick** (animation, WS3, severity 3, effort S, expected gain +0.3)
   - Problem: the bumpers flash but nothing moves (`strips/S02-bumpers`).
   - Fix: on a hit, drop the skirt ring 2–3 mm for 2–3 frames and squash or flash the cap. Add a small camera nudge on hard hits.
   - Test: ring displacement is visible between consecutive S02 frames.
10. **G-brass-guides** (toys, WS2, severity 2, effort M, expected gain +0.3)
    - Problem: the lane guides are thick brass cylinders (`desktop/C04-vapors`, `desktop/C06-arlington`).
    - Fix: replace them with thin stainless wire guides or flat metal guides on posts, with rubber rings.
    - Test: guide diameter is under half the ball's diameter in C04.
11. **G-mobile-steam-dots** (animation, WS3, severity 2, effort S, expected gain +0.2)
    - Problem: phone steam is still small dots (`mobile/R08-wizard`, `mobile/R11-landscape`).
    - Fix: scale the puff sprites by DPR and tier so the phone matches desktop.
    - Test: no puff is under 20 device px in `mobile/R08-wizard`.
12. **G-superior-plate** (typography, WS4, severity 2, effort S, expected gain +0.2)
    - Problem: the SUPERIOR plate is hidden behind the Vapors marquee (`desktop/C01-row`: "SU… HALE").
    - Fix: move the plate inboard or below the marquee's footprint.
    - Test: the full word is visible in C01 and C04.
13. **G-lower-field-art** (art, WS4, severity 2, effort L, expected gain +0.3)
    - Problem: the lower half is plain deco terrazzo, and the slings are flat colour (`desktop/C03-flippers`).
    - Fix: paint themed elements (bath-hall tile, steam motifs, the Promenade) into the lower field and decorate the sling plastics.
    - Test: no flat-fill area larger than one flipper's length remains in C03.
