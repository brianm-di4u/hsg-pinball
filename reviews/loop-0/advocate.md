# Advocate review: Spa City Pinball, loop 0 (tier high)

Capture paths are relative to `games/Pinball/screenshots/review-loop-0/`. I opened every capture I cite at full size, plus all three strips and the mobile contact sheet. I cropped and enlarged three regions to check specific claims: the backglass in `desktop/r03-play`, the ball in `desktop/r03-play`, and the spinner in `mobile/r07-mode`.

Critic means are: art 5.75, toys 5.0, lighting 5.5, animation 6.0, ui 5.75, typography 5.75, readability 5.25, mobile 4.75 and authenticity 6.25, for a total of 50.0. Most of the critics' findings hold up against the captures. My corrections fall into three groups:
- **Wrong or misplaced facts.** Critic A's reading of the backglass is wrong, and so is Codex's claim that the ramp is opaque. A few defects are pinned to the wrong cause or the wrong frame.
- **Close-up artefacts treated as game defects.** Some problems only appear in the close-up cameras and never in the player's view.
- **Untested items treated as missing.** Some features are called absent when no capture could have shown them.

None of this lifts any category to its gate. My proposed total is **51.0**.

## Facts a critic got plainly wrong

1. **Critic A says the backglass "reads as the Riviera".** This is wrong in substance. The enlarged backglass in `desktop/r03-play` (left panel, also in `desktop/r02-serve`) shows four local landmarks: the lattice Mountain Tower on the ridge, a twin-towered Mediterranean-revival hotel (the Arlington's silhouette), a mosaic-tiled dome, and steam rising out of the wooded mountainside. The magnolias in the foreground are a Southern plant. The palms are the only Riviera element, and they are a real error. Codex read the same panel correctly ("bathhouse architecture, wooded hills and the tower").
2. **Codex calls the ramp opaque ("faceted grey trough", "make the ramp visibly clear").** `desktop/c05-tower` shows a clear plastic ramp: the tower's lattice and the amber floor stripe are visible through its side wall, and so is the painted field's edge. The faceting is real; the opacity is not. At full-table framing (`desktop/r03-play`) the ramp does read milky, which is a lighting and material problem, not a transparency one.
3. **Critic A says the scoop's "VAPORS" lettering is hidden under its wall** (`desktop/c04-vapors`). It is only hidden from that oblique close-up angle. In the player's view, `mobile/r03-play` shows "THE VAPORS" lettered in red along the scoop's right wall at about (285, 890).
4. **Critic A says halos wash out "HALE and OZARK"** (`desktop/c01-row`). Both labels are legible in that capture. The label actually lost is SUPERIOR, under the left halo and the scoop wall. The underlying finding (halos are too big) stands, and `mobile/r08-wizard` proves it better.
5. **Critic A says the third DMD line "rides the bezel"** (`desktop/r06-multiball`). "11 MONTHS" sits inside the dot field with a visible margin, in both `desktop/r06-multiball` and `mobile/r06-multiball`. Three lines is cramped; nothing overlaps the bezel.
6. **Codex says `desktop/r04-bumpers` places the ball "among similarly bright scenic detail" in the bumpers.** The ball in that frame is at the top arch, at about (595, 75), above the bumpers and not among them. The read problem is real, but it is against the spring painting at the top, not in the bumper nest.
7. **Codex says the phone has only keyboard-first guidance** (`mobile/r09-help`). `mobile/r01-title` ends with "Tap the left or right side to flip". The touch instruction exists on the title card; it is the help screen's ordering that is wrong.

## Category by category

### Art (critics 5.5 / 6; mean 5.75): propose 6.0
**Conceded:**
- There are hard horizontal seams: in `desktop/c01-row` at y≈285 (right side) and y≈600, and in `desktop/c03-flippers` at y≈215 under the multiplier inserts.
- The apron is a flat navy band (`desktop/r03-play`, y≈740–790).
- The unlit Row targets are plain grey frames (`desktop/c01-row`).

**Rebutted in part:**
- The "near-empty lower third" is overstated. In `desktop/r03-play` the deco fan and promenade art carry down to y≈590, and they frame the mode inserts and the "VALLEY OF THE VAPORS" arc. In `desktop/c03-flippers` the flipper well still carries the fan's radial rays. It is dim, not blank.
- At gameplay framing the upper seam is mostly masked by the Row guide wall (`desktop/r03-play`, y≈290).
- The critics give too little weight to the fact that the backglass and the playfield are visibly in the same hand. Compare the palette and painting style of `desktop/r02-serve` (backglass) with `desktop/c02-springs` (playfield).
- The "SPA CITY / BATHHOUSE ROW · HOT SPRINGS" lettering and the gold pinstripes are integrated into the layout (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/r03-play`).

This is above the 5 anchor's "rectangles unrelated to the layout" and short of 7. 6.0 is within +0.25 of the mean.

### Toys (critics 5 / 5; mean 5.0): propose 5.0
**Conceded:**
- The Arlington is a red roof with two dormers (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
- The tower is a small lattice inside the ramp that disappears at full framing (`desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/r03-play`).
- The Vapors sign is a thumb-sized neon strip (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
- The guides are flat dark boxes with flat gold caps, and the rubber posts render as white crescents (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).

**Rebutted:**
- The ramp is clear plastic, not opaque (fact 2 above).
- The bathhouses are more differentiated than "toy blocks". `desktop/c06-arlington` shows a mosaic Quapaw dome, the Ozark's twin red-capped towers, and distinct roof colours and window grids along the Row.

**Evidence the critics underweight:**
- The wireform chrome is convincing (`desktop/c05-tower`).
- The ball is a true chrome mirror in `desktop/c03-flippers`, where it reflects the table.
- The bumpers have chrome skirts and decal caps (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- The sling plastics have a layered inner chevron (`desktop/c03-flippers`).

The failures in the first list still put the category at the 5 anchor. I hold 5.0.

### Lighting (critics 5.5 / 5.5; mean 5.5): propose 5.5
**Conceded:**
- The halos are oversized. All eight Row lamps lit in `mobile/r08-wizard` merge into a white bar that hides the bathhouse names.
- There are no visible contact shadows under the bumpers or bathhouses (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/c01-row`).
- The GI is even and flat.
- Neither `desktop/r07-mode` nor `desktop/r08-wizard` shifts the mood. The wizard lights more lamps (every Row target, all four multipliers, the blue Central Avenue lamps) but the GI does not change.

**Rebutted on severity:**
- The "blue-white glare" exists only in the close-up cameras (`desktop/c02-springs` top corners, `desktop/c06-arlington` left). It does not appear in any player-view frame: `desktop/r02-serve` through `desktop/r08-wizard`, or `mobile/r02-serve` through `mobile/r08-wizard`. It is a close-up camera artefact, not something a player sees.
- `strips/s03-multiball` shows the critics' own credit is deserved: there is a chase across the Row, the purple mode arc washes up, and amber and blue edge flashes alternate between frames 0–3.

Score unchanged.

### Animation (critics 6 / 6; mean 6.0): propose 6.0
**Conceded:**
- The flipper return cycle is not shown: the flipper stays up through frames 4–7 of `strips/s01-flip`.
- The steam reads as dot clusters (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`).
- No bumper skirt displacement can be resolved at strip scale (`strips/s02-bumpers`).

**Rebutted on cause:**
- Critic A lists "no sling kick" and "no camera reaction" as missing. Neither strip contains a sling hit or a nudge: `strips/s01-flip` is a flipper shot from the left inlane, and `strips/s02-bumpers` is a bumper sequence. These features are untested, not shown to be absent. They earn no credit, but the gap should ask for a capture as well as a fix.
- Critic A's "static DERBY DAY" in `desktop/r07-mode` is one still frame, which cannot show whether the DMD animates.

**Positive evidence:**
- The flipper snaps in one step (frames 2→3 of `strips/s01-flip`).
- The bumper caps flash and then decay: in `strips/s02-bumpers` all three are lit in frame 0, and the top-right cap is dark by frame 6.
- The steam rises.
- The light show runs (`strips/s03-multiball`).

That is most of the 7 anchor's list, but without the follow-through. I hold 6.0.

### UI (critics 5.5 / 6; mean 5.75): propose 6.0
**Conceded:**
- The wizard DMD repeats itself ("VALLEY OF THE VAPORS / NICKNAME: VALLEY OF THE VAPORS") in `desktop/r08-wizard` and `mobile/r08-wizard`.
- During multiball the DMD shows trivia instead of a jackpot instruction (`desktop/r06-multiball`).
- On the phone, the score disappears during a mode callout (`mobile/r07-mode`).
- The desktop help screen clips its Back button (`desktop/r09-help`).
- The phone help screen shows no close control (`mobile/r09-help`).
- The desktop right column is empty below y≈540 (`desktop/r03-play`).

**Rebutted:**
- Codex says the desktop layout wastes space on the backglass and a duplicated score "while keeping the table narrow". The table already uses the full 800 px height in `desktop/r03-play`, so its width is set by the aspect ratio, not taken by the backglass. The duplicated score under the DMD is exactly what keeps the score visible on desktop during callouts (`desktop/r07-mode` shows 1,274,560 under "DERBY DAY"), which is the very thing Codex faults the phone for lacking.
- The menus are consistent and clean (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r10-scores`, `mobile/r01-title`).
- The DMD's dot rendering and gold bezel have real character (`mobile/r03-play`).

6.0 is within +0.25 of the mean.

### Typography (critics 5.5 / 6; mean 5.75): propose 5.5
**Conceded, with a defect neither critic fully caught:**
- The mode labels under the purple inserts are illegible at gameplay size (`desktop/r03-play`, `mobile/r03-play`).
- SUPERIOR is clipped (`desktop/c01-row`).
- The bumper caps' "143°" has a malformed first glyph that reads like "r43°" (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- Codex is right that the spinner face is mirrored. Enlarged, `mobile/r07-mode` reads "5TH SEASON" in reverse.

**Rebutted:**
- The hidden VAPORS lettering (fact 3 above).
- Critic A calls the UI body "a generic system sans". `desktop/r09-help` and `mobile/r09-help` show a clean humanist sans paired with the slab display face for headings, and a still image cannot establish whether it is a system font.

**What works:** the display face (`desktop/r01-title`, `mobile/r01-title`) and the DMD headline (`mobile/r07-mode`).

The playfield lettering failures are numerous enough that I side with the lower score.

### Readability (critics 5 / 5.5; mean 5.25): propose 5.5
**Conceded:**
- The ball is a near-transparent glass bubble among the bumpers (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`, at about (530, 360)).
- It is hard to find against the spring painting at the top arch (`desktop/r04-bumpers`).
- The skill-shot lane is a single small lamp (`desktop/r02-serve`).
- Halos erase local detail in the wizard frame (`mobile/r08-wizard`).

**Rebutted in part:**
- Critic A's "dark chrome disc against dark art" in `desktop/r03-play` is overstated. Enlarged, the ball is a mid-grey chrome sphere with a clear top highlight against the brown fan art, and it is easy to find.
- On the phone ramp (`mobile/r05-ramp`, at about (655, 650)) the ball keeps a dark reflective rim that separates it from the pale plastic.
- All three multiball balls are findable at a glance (`desktop/r06-multiball`: top arch, between the flippers, shooter lane), and so is the ball in `mobile/r08-wizard` at the top right.
- Both critics accept that the lamp shape coding works (`desktop/r07-mode`).

The failures are local (the bumpers, the top painting) rather than general, which justifies 5.5.

### Mobile (critics 5 / 4.5; mean 4.75): propose 5.0
**Conceded in full:**
- Landscape fails. The table takes about 450 of 1688 px, roughly 27% of the width, in `mobile/r11-landscape`, while the DMD and a duplicate score fill the rest.
- The phone score disappears during modes (`mobile/r07-mode`).
- The phone help screen has no visible close control (`mobile/r09-help`).

**Rebutted:**
- Critic A says the Launch button sits over the shooter lane. In `mobile/r06-multiball` and `mobile/r08-wizard`, Launch covers the plunger housing below the ball. The resting ball, at about (735, 1500), is clear of the button, which starts at y≈1550.
- Launch only appears while a ball is in the plunger lane. It is absent from `mobile/r03-play`, `mobile/r04-bumpers`, `mobile/r05-ramp` and `mobile/r07-mode`. It does not breach the "never cover the ball's area" rule.
- Portrait is framed deliberately: the full table, a clear DMD band and no permanent flipper buttons (`mobile/r03-play`). The title and score cards scale cleanly (`mobile/r01-title`, `mobile/r10-scores`).

5.0 is within +0.25 of the mean.

### Authenticity (critics 6 / 6.5; mean 6.25): propose 6.5
**Conceded:**
- The springs painting's turquoise travertine terraces read as Pamukkale, not Hot Springs (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- The backglass palms are wrong.
- The Arlington, tower and Vapors are under-modelled as toys (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/c04-vapors`).
- "Arlington rebuilt in 11 months" is unhedged (`desktop/r06-multiball`).

**Rebutted:** the backglass carries the Mountain Tower, a twin-towered Arlington silhouette, a mosaic dome, and steam on a wooded ridge (fact 1 above; `desktop/r03-play`, `desktop/r02-serve`). Those landmarks are present in the game's art even where the toys fail.

**Further evidence:**
- The eight bathhouses are in the correct order (`desktop/c01-row`).
- The local street and lane names appear on the playfield (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c05-tower`).
- The About text hedges its facts (`desktop/r09-help`).

6.5 is within +0.25 of the mean.
