# Prosecutor review: Spa City Pinball, loop 0 (tier high)

Critic means (A / B → mean): art 5.5/6 → 5.75; toys 5/5 → 5.0; lighting 5.5/5.5 → 5.5; animation 6/6 → 6.0; ui 5.5/6 → 5.75;
typography 5.5/6 → 5.75; readability 5/5.5 → 5.25; mobile 5/4.5 → 4.75; authenticity 6/6.5 → 6.25. Total of the means: 50.0.

I opened every full-size frame, all six close-ups, all three strips and the individual strip frames (`desktop/s01-*`,
`desktop/s02-*`, `desktop/s03-*`), and zoomed into crops of the close-ups and the phone frames. In nearly every
category the critics recorded the right defects and then scored as if the next anchor up were half met. Several defects
are missing from both reviews: mirrored playfield text in gameplay frames, a ball drawn in the same visual vocabulary as
the steam particles, slingshots with no rubber, a phone DMD that hides the score during ordinary play, mode inserts with
four brightness levels instead of a lit/unlit pair, and flasher bloom spilling into the black void around the cabinet.

A note on the captures. `desktop/r04-bumpers` and `mobile/r04-bumpers` are labelled "ball in the pop bumpers", but in
both the ball sits above the bumpers near the top arch, and none of the three caps is flashing. In `desktop/s03-multiball-0`,
the first frame of a "multiball launch" strip, I can't find a ball anywhere. I score what is visible, so those frames
can't be credited with bumper action or a multiball read.

## Category by category

### art: critics 5.5 / 6 (mean 5.75). Proposed 5.0
The rubric's 5 anchor reads "coherent palette, but art sits in rectangles unrelated to the layout; empty patches". That
describes the playfield exactly. Good paintings don't lift the score to 6 when the anchor is about composition.
- `desktop/c01-row`: two ruler-straight horizontal seams run across the table. At y≈285 the spring painting stops dead
  against hex tile, and at y≈600 the hex tile stops dead against the promenade. Neither follows a guide wall or an
  inlay line.
- `desktop/c03-flippers`: a third straight seam at y≈215 directly under the 2X–5X inserts. Below it, the entire flipper
  zone (y≈215–765, about 70% of the frame) is flat navy with faint radial lines. The apron (y≈765–800) is a blank
  navy band with no cards, art or lettering.
- `desktop/r03-play`: the right third of the playfield below the S·P·A lanes is taken up by the grey ramp and its
  return. The ramp carries no decal or plastic art, and the painting beneath it isn't composed around it.
- `desktop/c01-row`: unlit Row targets are grey outlined rectangles that look like placeholders. They aren't art inserts.
- The backglass (`desktop/r03-play` left panel) is attractive and in the same hand. It is a static painting with a
  title floated over its top border. Nothing frames the art or lights it.
Codex's 6 credits "beyond decorative rectangles alone". In c01 and c03 the zones are rectangles. 5.0.

### toys: critics 5 / 5 (mean 5.0). Proposed 4.5
At 5, "recognisable models in flat materials" requires the models to be recognisable. Several aren't, and the
materials and hardware fall short of 5 in places the critics didn't open.
- **Unrecognisable landmarks.** In `desktop/c06-arlington` the Arlington is a red roof with two dormers, peeking over a
  black box. In `desktop/c05-tower` the Mountain Tower is a 30 px lattice trapped inside the ramp. In `desktop/c04-vapors`
  (zoomed), the Vapors "sign" is a tiny vertical neon strip whose letters can't be read even in the close-up, standing
  beside a grey box. Three of the four named hero toys fail "reads at a glance".
- **Slingshots have no rubber** (`desktop/c03-flippers`). Each sling is a coloured plastic triangle on a white base.
  There is no rubber band on the kicking face and there are no sling posts. Neither critic mentioned this.
- **Pop bumpers** (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/s02-bumpers-1`) are a peach cylinder between a cap and a chrome base.
  There is no skirt, no metal ring and no rubber.
- **Posts float.** In `desktop/c04-vapors` and `desktop/c06-arlington` each rubber post is a grey cap on a wall top,
  with two disconnected white crescents at the base. They read as a rendering artefact, not a rubber ring.
- **Stray geometry.** `desktop/c06-arlington` at about x 975, y 580 shows a stray orange rod standing on the gold
  wall cap.
- **Ramp.** In `desktop/c05-tower` the ramp walls are visibly stepped segments. The ramp and wireform run off the
  table edge over a pure black void, with no cabinet side and no ramp entry flap.
- **Ball.** `desktop/s02-bumpers-1` and `desktop/r05-ramp` (zoomed) show a translucent glass bubble, not chrome.
  Only `desktop/c03-flippers` shows a chrome ball.
- **What works:** wireform chrome, the bathhouse silhouettes (Quapaw dome, Ozark towers) in `desktop/c01-row`, and the
  scoop rims.
The anchor-5 rails-as-blocks description plus unrecognisable hero toys and missing sling rubber put this at 4.5.

### lighting: critics 5.5 / 5.5 (mean 5.5). Proposed 5.0
The 5 anchor reads "lamps light but look pasted on; washed-out or blown areas". The captures show blown areas
repeatedly.
- **Halos merge into blown bars.** In `desktop/r08-wizard` and `mobile/r08-wizard` the eight Row target halos fuse into
  a single white band that buries the bathhouse names and the models above them. In `desktop/s03-multiball-5` the six
  mode-insert halos fuse into one purple arc, with the labels gone.
- **Perimeter glare.** A blue-white smear runs along the upper rail in `desktop/c02-springs` and at the left edge of
  `desktop/c06-arlington`.
- **Light spills off the table.** In `desktop/s03-multiball-0` and `strips/s03-multiball` frames 0 and 7, an amber
  flasher glow sits in the black void beyond the cabinet (top-right). Light with no surface to land on looks pasted on.
- **No grounding.** No toy casts a visible contact shadow: not the bathhouses (`desktop/c01-row`) and not the bumpers
  (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **No mood change.** The GI is the same in `desktop/r03-play`, `desktop/r06-multiball`, `desktop/r07-mode` and
  `desktop/r08-wizard`. The "wizard light show" is the play frame with more inserts on. The ball's reflection works
  only in `desktop/c03-flippers`; elsewhere it shows transmission (see readability).
Nothing reaches the 7 anchor ("bloom only where lit… no glare"), and the blown areas are the 5 description. 5.0.

### animation: critics 6 / 6 (mean 6.0). Proposed 5.5
The 7 anchor needs flipper snap, bumpers that flash and drop, steam, light shows and a camera that reacts. What the
strips show:
- `strips/s01-flip`: the flipper jumps up in one step between frames 2 and 3, then stays up through frame 7. No
  slingshot fires, no insert changes state across all 8 frames, and the camera doesn't react.
- `strips/s02-bumpers`: the bumper bodies glow and decay, which is real. The caps and skirts never move, so there is
  no "drop". The steam is discrete round bubbles drifting upward (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`).
- `strips/s03-multiball`: an insert chase runs (Row, then the mode arc). But the launch itself isn't visible: no ball
  in frame 0 (`desktop/s03-multiball-0`) and at most one ball in later frames (`desktop/s03-multiball-5`, ball at about
  x 965, y 615). No toy moves.
- The DMD animations listed in the brief (slot reels, derby race, tower) appear in no capture. Derby Day is static
  text in `desktop/r07-mode` and `mobile/r07-mode`.
- Both critics said "close to 7". Of the anchor's five items, two are met (flipper snap, a light chase), one is half
  met (flash without drop), steam reads as bubbles rather than steam, and the camera does nothing.
5.5.

### ui: critics 5.5 / 6 (mean 5.75). Proposed 5.0
The DMD rendering and the menu card style are good. The DMD's content and the HUD hierarchy are not.
- **The phone hides the score in ordinary play.** `mobile/r04-bumpers` and `mobile/r05-ramp` show "SPA CITY / BALL 1"
  with no score anywhere on screen, and `mobile/r07-mode` shows only "DERBY DAY". The critics flagged only the mode
  frame. Two of the three ordinary-play phone frames have no score either.
- **No callouts during action.** The DMD has none in `desktop/r04-bumpers` or `desktop/r05-ramp`.
- **Wizard copy.** `desktop/r08-wizard` repeats itself: "VALLEY OF THE VAPORS / NICKNAME: VALLEY OF THE VAPORS".
- **Multiball.** `desktop/r06-multiball` and `mobile/r06-multiball` squeeze three lines into the DMD, and the top line
  touches the bezel. The text is trivia, not a jackpot instruction.
- **Stale DMD behind menus.** `desktop/r10-scores` and `mobile/r10-scores` show the wizard's "VALLEY OF THE VAPORS"
  still on the DMD behind the High scores menu.
- **Help screen.** `desktop/r09-help` clips its Back button to a sliver, and `mobile/r09-help` has no visible close
  control.
- **Desktop layout.** `desktop/r03-play` repeats the score (DMD plus HUD) and leaves the right column empty below
  y≈540, while the table gets about 36% of the width.
The anchor-7 items "readable animations" and "nothing overlapping" both fail. 5.0.

### typography: critics 5.5 / 6 (mean 5.75). Proposed 5.0
The display slab serif is good (`desktop/r01-title`, `mobile/r01-title`). The playfield lettering has errors beyond
small size that neither critic counted against the score:
- **Mirrored text in gameplay frames.** The spinner flag reads "5TH SEASON" backwards from the player's camera in
  `mobile/r04-bumpers` (zoomed: "NOSAES HT5"), `mobile/r05-ramp`, `mobile/r07-mode`, `desktop/s03-multiball-0`
  (x≈360, y≈238) and `desktop/c04-vapors` ("EASON" reversed). Codex saw it only in the close-up. It is in every
  gameplay view.
- **Wrong glyph.** The bumper decal "143°" renders with a wrong first glyph, so it reads roughly "ı43°" or "r43o" on all three caps
  (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/s02-bumpers-1`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).
- **Illegible mode labels.** On the phone the labels are about 8 device px, and the lit insert's halo erases "SOAK"
  (`mobile/r03-play`, zoomed). "TUBS" on the playfield doesn't match "TUB RACE" in `desktop/r09-help`.
- **Clipped or hidden lettering.** In `desktop/c01-row` SUPERIOR is cut to "…OR" under a halo. The scoop's own VAPORS
  lettering runs sideways along its wall (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
- **Body face.** The menus use a generic system sans (`desktop/r09-help`).
Mirrored and misspelled playfield text is a defect, not just a "mixed scale". 5.0.

### readability: critics 5 / 5.5 (mean 5.25). Proposed 4.5
"5: the ball gets lost in the art." It does, and it also gets confused with the effects.
- **The ball looks like a steam particle.** In `desktop/s02-bumpers-1` and `desktop/r04-bumpers` (zoomed) the ball is
  a translucent bubble with a white highlight. The steam drifting around it is translucent bubbles with white
  highlights. In the bumper zone the ball has no unique visual signature.
- **Ramp.** In `desktop/r05-ramp` (zoomed, x≈785, y≈240) the glass ball on pale ramp plastic is near zero contrast.
  `mobile/r05-ramp` is the same.
- **Ball size.** On desktop the table spans about x 410–870 of 1280, and the ball is about 11 px (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **Multiball.** `desktop/s03-multiball-0` shows no locatable ball. `desktop/s03-multiball-5` shows one, and
  `desktop/r06-multiball` shows three only on close search: one at the top arch, one between the flippers, one in the
  shooter lane.
- **Lit vs unlit is not binary.** The mode inserts in `mobile/r03-play` (zoomed) show four levels: SOAK blazing,
  DERBY pale lavender, RAID mid-purple, TOWER/TUBS/COASTER dark. I can't tell which are "lit".
- **Wizard.** In `mobile/r08-wizard` and `desktop/r08-wizard` the Row targets become one white band.
- **Skill shot.** The lit lane in `desktop/r02-serve` is one small dot.
- **What works:** the shape coding (arrows, circles, triangles).
4.5.

### mobile: critics 5 / 4.5 (mean 4.75). Proposed 4.0
- **Landscape.** `mobile/r11-landscape` is an outright failure. The table spans x 0–450 of 1688 device px (27%) and
  is jammed against the left edge with no margin. A DMD about three times the table's width, plus a duplicate score,
  fills the rest.
- **Launch over the shooter lane.** "Touch controls never cover the ball's area": in `mobile/r02-serve`,
  `mobile/r06-multiball` and `mobile/r08-wizard` the Launch button (x≈610–760, y≈1550–1660) sits on the shooter lane and
  plunger, directly under the served ball (x≈735, y≈1500).
- **No score.** Score is missing during ordinary play on the phone (`mobile/r04-bumpers`, `mobile/r05-ramp`) and in
  mode (`mobile/r07-mode`).
- **Help.** `mobile/r09-help` leads with keyboard keys, cuts off mid-sentence and offers no close control.
- **Labels.** At 390 px portrait the bathhouse and mode labels can't be read (`mobile/r03-play`).
- **What works:** portrait framing is sensible (`mobile/r03-play`), and the menus scale well (`mobile/r10-scores`).
One of the two required orientations fails outright, and the other has a control on the ball's path. 4.0.

### authenticity: critics 6 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed 5.5
- **Positive:** the Row is in correct order, Superior to Lamar, but only legible in the close-up `desktop/c01-row`.
  At play size the names are unreadable (`mobile/r03-play`), so at the gameplay camera the Row is "eight little
  buildings". The help text is local and hedged (`desktop/r09-help`).
- **Wrong place in the paintings.** The largest painting on the table, the top third, shows turquoise travertine
  terraces (`desktop/c02-springs`). They read as Pamukkale, not Hot Springs Mountain's tufa and wooded slopes. The
  backglass is lined with palms (`desktop/r03-play`, left panel).
- **Missing landmarks.** The Arlington, the tower and the Vapors aren't recognisable as models (`desktop/c06-arlington`,
  `desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/c04-vapors`). Codex itself wrote "labels carry too much of the local identity".
- **Unhedged fact.** "ARLINGTON REBUILT IN 11 MONTHS" on the DMD (`desktop/r06-multiball`) carries no "about" or
  "reportedly".
The 7 anchor needs the tower, the Arlington and the Vapors recognisable. All three fail. With the Row only readable
in close-up, this sits at the 6 gate or just under it, not above it. 5.5.

## IP check
I checked this myself and found it clean. There are no commercial pinball machine names, titles, logos or art. I
zoomed into the backglass (`desktop/r03-play`, left panel) and found no lettering or pseudo-lettering. The neon canopy
at the right is shapes only. I found no text in the spring painting (`desktop/c02-springs`) or the promenade
(`desktop/c01-row`). All lettering is code-drawn: the inserts, street names, "SPA CITY", the bumper caps and the
mirrored spinner flag. The real names, the Arlington and the Vapors, appear as text labels and a toy sign. I saw no
reproduced business logo.

## Proposed scores
art 5.0, toys 4.5, lighting 5.0, animation 5.5, ui 5.0, typography 5.0, readability 4.5, mobile 4.0, authenticity 5.5.
**Total 44.0 / 90** (critic mean 50.0). Every category is below its gate.

## Added gaps (missed or under-weighted by both critics)
1. **G-ball-vs-steam** (readability, WS1, severity 4, effort S, +0.5). The ball and the steam share the
   translucent-bubble look (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`, `desktop/r04-bumpers`). Fix: make the ball opaque chrome (no
   transmission, metalness 1, env map) with a dark rim, and draw steam as soft-edged wisps at alpha ≤0.35 with no
   specular. Test: in every `s02` frame, the ball is the only object with a hard specular highlight and a dark edge.
2. **G-mirrored-playfield-text** (typography, WS4, severity 3, effort S, +0.5). The spinner flag "5TH SEASON" reads
   backwards in `mobile/r04-bumpers`, `mobile/r07-mode`, `desktop/s03-multiball-0` and `desktop/c04-vapors`. The bumper
   "143°" has a wrong first glyph (`desktop/c02-springs`). Fix: flip the flag texture on its player-facing side, and
   render the cap decal from a font that has the glyphs. Test: both read left to right, correctly spelled, in the
   frames named.
3. **G-phone-score-always** (ui, WS5, severity 3, effort S, +0.5). The phone DMD shows no score in `mobile/r04-bumpers`,
   `mobile/r05-ramp` or `mobile/r07-mode`. Fix: the DMD idle state is score plus ball. Callouts last ≤2 s and then
   return to score, or keep a score strip under the callout. Test: score is visible in every phone gameplay frame.
4. **G-sling-bumper-hardware** (toys, WS2, severity 3, effort M, +0.5). Slings have no rubber (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
   Bumpers have no skirt or ring (`desktop/c02-springs`). Posts are floating crescents (`desktop/c04-vapors`). A stray
   rod stands on a wall cap (`desktop/c06-arlington` at about x 975, y 580). Fix: model sling rubbers stretched over
   three posts, bumper skirts plus chrome rings, and torus post rubbers; delete the stray mesh. Test: c03 and c06 show
   continuous rubber on every post and sling.
5. **G-binary-insert-states** (readability, WS1, severity 3, effort S, +0.5). The mode inserts show four brightness
   levels (`mobile/r03-play`). Fix: off means a dim tinted insert with a readable label; on means full brightness plus
   halo; blinking means in progress. Test: the insert luminance histogram in r03 clusters into ≤2 steady states, and
   the label under a lit insert stays legible.
6. **G-desktop-table-scale** (readability, WS6, severity 3, effort M, +0.5). At 1280×800 the table uses about 36% of
   the width, the ball is about 11 px, and the right column is empty below y≈540 (`desktop/r03-play`). Fix: enlarge
   the table, for example by narrowing the backglass panel and stacking the DMD over it. Test: ball ≥16 px and table
   ≥45% of the viewport width in r03.
7. **G-offtable-spill** (lighting, WS1, severity 2, effort M, +0.25). Flasher bloom glows in the black void beyond the
   cabinet (`desktop/s03-multiball-0`), and the ramp and wireform run over the void (`desktop/c05-tower`). Fix: model
   the cabinet side walls and lockdown rails, set a dim room backdrop, and keep flasher lights inside the table bounds.
   Test: no bloom pixels outside the cabinet silhouette in the s03 frames.
