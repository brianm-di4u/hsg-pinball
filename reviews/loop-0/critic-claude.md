# Critic A review: Spa City Pinball, loop 0 (tier high)

## Verdict
The table has a real identity. The backglass painting is strong, the thermal-spring painting at the top is detailed, and the amber dot-matrix DMD with its gold bezel has character. Bathhouse Row is spelled out in the correct north-to-south order. Next to Pinball FX or a good VPX table, though, it reads as a solid prototype and not a finished machine. Much of the 3D is boxy and untextured: guide walls are flat dark slabs with flat gold tops, and the rubber posts render as white crescents. The Arlington is a strip of red roof, and the Mountain Tower is a ghost inside the ramp. The ball is small and low in contrast, and in the bumpers it turns into a near-transparent glass bubble. The painting zones meet at hard straight seams, and the lower third of the playfield is close to empty navy. Modes and the wizard mode don't change the mood of the lighting. Phone landscape squeezes the table into the left quarter of the screen. Every category is below its gate except authenticity, which sits exactly on its gate.

## Scores

**art: 5.5.** Each painting is good on its own: the springs terraces (`desktop/c02-springs`), the promenade with its bench (`desktop/c01-row`) and the deco fan behind the inserts (`desktop/r03-play`). The backglass (`desktop/r03-play`, left panel) looks like the same hand. But the zones are masked rectangles with hard horizontal seams: in `desktop/c01-row` there is a straight edge across the table at y≈285 (mountain to hex tile) and at y≈600 (hex tile to promenade). Below the insert row the field is almost blank dark navy with faint radial lines (`desktop/c03-flippers`, `desktop/r03-play` from y≈600 down), and the apron is a flat navy band (`desktop/r03-play` y≈740–790, `mobile/r03-play`). The unlit Row targets are plain grey rectangles that look like placeholders (`desktop/c01-row`). This is the anchor-5 description ("art sits in rectangles… empty patches") with better-than-5 paintings.

**toys: 5.0.**
- **Bathhouses:** they read as bathhouses in a toy-block way. The Quapaw dome and Ozark twin towers are good nods (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c06-arlington`). But they are tiny, flat-shaded and unbevelled, and they perch on top of a guide wall.
- **Arlington:** reduced to a red roof with two dormers behind the lock saucer (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
- **Mountain Tower:** a small faint lattice inside the ramp entrance, invisible at full-table framing (`desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/r05-ramp`).
- **Vapors sign:** a sliver of neon beside a small box inside the scoop (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
- **Guides and posts:** the guides are dark boxes with flat gold caps, which is exactly anchor 5's "rails read as blocks" (`desktop/c04-vapors`). Rubber posts render as pairs of white crescents (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).
- **Ramp:** clear but its walls are visibly stepped and segmented (`desktop/c05-tower`).
- **What works:** the wireform chrome is convincing (`desktop/c05-tower`). Flippers are clean, but they are plain white bats with a red rubber band and no decal (`desktop/c03-flippers`). The bumpers are solid, with chrome bases and teal caps (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Ball:** chrome in `desktop/c03-flippers`, but glassy in `desktop/s02-bumpers-1`.

**lighting: 5.5.**
- **Bloom:** mostly limited to the lamps, which is right. But the halos are oversized discs that wash out the labels under them: HALE and OZARK in `desktop/c01-row`, the purple arc in `desktop/s03-multiball-5`.
- **Glare:** a blue-white glare streak sits top-left in `desktop/c02-springs` and `desktop/c06-arlington`.
- **GI:** the field is evenly lit with no warm pools, and the lower playfield falls into murk (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
- **Shadows:** toys barely ground themselves; the bathhouses and bumpers cast no visible contact shadow (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Modes:** the wizard frame `desktop/r08-wizard` is normal GI with more inserts lit, and Derby Day `desktop/r07-mode` has no tint. There is no mood shift.
- **What works:** the multiball light show (`strips/s03-multiball`) sweeps the inserts and flashes colour at the screen edges. The ball does reflect the table (`desktop/c03-flippers`).

**animation: 6.0.**
- **Works in the strips:** the flipper snaps in one step between frames 2 and 3 of `strips/s01-flip`, and the ball's path reads. In `strips/s02-bumpers` the bumper caps flash per hit and steam particles rise. In `strips/s03-multiball` an insert chase runs across the Row, then the mode arc.
- **Missing:** the slingshots show no visible kick, the camera shows no visible reaction, and the bumper skirts don't visibly drop.
- **Steam:** it reads as clusters of bubble dots, not wisps (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`).
- **DMD:** the mode capture shows a static "DERBY DAY" with no race animation (`desktop/r07-mode`).
- Close to the 7 anchor, but not there.

**ui: 5.5.** The DMD has real dot rendering, an amber phosphor look and a gold bezel. The menus share the gold-bordered card style (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r10-scores`). The problems:
- **DMD copy:** `desktop/r08-wizard` repeats itself ("VALLEY OF THE VAPORS / NICKNAME: VALLEY OF THE VAPORS"). `desktop/r06-multiball` crams three lines and the last line rides the bezel. `desktop/r04-bumpers` and `desktop/r05-ramp` show the generic "SPA CITY / BALL 1" during action, with no callout.
- **Help screen:** at 1280×800 the help modal cuts off its Back button at the bottom edge (`desktop/r09-help`). The phone help screen shows no close control at all (`mobile/r09-help`).
- **Desktop layout:** the right column is empty below y≈540 (`desktop/r03-play`).

**typography: 5.5.** The display face, a glowing slab serif for PINBALL and "SPA CITY" on the playfield, is deliberate and good (`desktop/r01-title`, `mobile/r01-title`). The UI body is a generic system sans (`desktop/r09-help`). The DMD 5×7 font is clean at two lines but gets cramped at three (`desktop/r06-multiball`). Playfield lettering:
- The mode labels under the purple inserts (SOAK, DERBY and so on) can't be read on either viewport (`desktop/r03-play`, `mobile/r03-play`).
- "MOUNTAIN TOWER" is overprinted by its lamps (`desktop/r03-play`).
- "SUPERIOR" is clipped by the left scoop (`desktop/c01-row`).
- The scoop's own "VAPORS" lettering is hidden under its wall (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
- The bumper caps' "143°" renders with an odd first glyph (`desktop/c02-springs`).

**readability: 5.0.**
- **Ball:** about 10 px on desktop, and a dark chrome disc against dark art (`desktop/r03-play`). In the bumpers it is nearly transparent glass (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`). On the ramp it is low in contrast against the pale plastic (`desktop/r05-ramp`). It gets lost in the art in frames 6–7 of `strips/s01-flip`.
- **Multiball:** the three balls can be found but don't pop (`desktop/r06-multiball`).
- **Skill shot:** the lit lane in `desktop/r02-serve` is one small lamp among the S·P·A guides, and the shot is hard to see.
- **Lamps:** the shape coding works, and lit vs unlit coloured inserts are distinguishable (`desktop/r07-mode`, `mobile/r07-mode`).

**mobile: 5.0.**
- **Portrait works:** the table fills the width and the DMD at the top is readable (`mobile/r03-play`, `mobile/r07-mode`).
- **Portrait problems:** the Launch button sits over the bottom of the shooter lane and the plunger (`mobile/r06-multiball`). The score disappears whenever the DMD shows a mode, and there is no separate score line (`mobile/r07-mode`). The insert labels can't be read.
- **Landscape fails:** the table takes the left ~27% of the screen while a giant DMD and buttons fill the rest (`mobile/r11-landscape`). That is the opposite of "framed on purpose".

**authenticity: 6.0.**
- **Strong in the text and layout:** eight bathhouses labelled in the correct order, Superior through Lamar (`desktop/c01-row`). Central Avenue, Reserve St and Thermal Springs lane names. 143 °F. Arlington, Vapors and Mountain Tower shots. Derby Day, Raid 1967 and "Valley of the Vapors" modes (`desktop/r09-help`). The About text hedges its facts properly.
- **Weaker in the visuals:** the springs painting shows turquoise travertine terraces that read as Pamukkale, not Hot Springs (`desktop/c02-springs`). The backglass is a palm-lined, neon-reflecting promenade that reads as the Riviera (`desktop/r03-play`). The landmark models that should sell the place are either generic or tiny: the Arlington and the tower (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c05-tower`). The "Arlington rebuilt in 11 months" DMD fact is unhedged (`desktop/r06-multiball`).

## IP check
Clean. I saw no commercial pinball title, logo or art, and no real business logo. I found no legible text in the playfield paintings or the backglass (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/r03-play`). All lettering is code-drawn: the inserts, street names and the bumper-cap "143°".

## Ranked gaps
1. **G-ball-contrast** (readability, WS1, severity 5, effort S, about +1.0). The ball turns glassy or dark against the art (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`, `desktop/r03-play`, `desktop/r05-ramp`). Fix: give the ball metalness 1 and roughness ≤0.1, raise env intensity, and add a soft contact shadow plus a small specular sprite. Test: ball-to-background luminance contrast ≥3:1 in r03, r05 and s02.
2. **G-mobile-landscape** (mobile, WS6, severity 5, effort M, about +1.0). In `mobile/r11-landscape` the table uses about 27% of the width. Fix: give the table the full height and centre it, and shrink the DMD into a ≤25%-width side panel. Test: the table is at least 40% of the viewport width.
3. **G-landmark-models** (toys and authenticity, WS2, severity 4, effort M, about +0.5 each). The Arlington is a roof strip and the tower is hidden (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c05-tower`). Fix: build a twin-towered Arlington standup behind the lock at ≥2× its current height, and move the Mountain Tower outside the ramp footprint at ≥3× its current height. Test: both identifiable in the full-table frame r03.
4. **G-wall-rubber-materials** (toys, WS2, severity 4, effort M, about +0.5). Box guides and white-crescent posts (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`). Fix: chamfer the guide edges, add a lacquer clearcoat and a brushed-brass normal map, and replace the crescents with torus rubber rings at roughness about 0.8.
5. **G-lower-playfield-empty** (art, WS4, severity 4, effort M, about +0.5). Bare navy below the inserts and a flat apron (`desktop/c03-flippers`, `desktop/r03-play`). Fix: carry the deco fan or promenade art down to the flipper returns, and add apron cards or illustration. Test: no flat-colour region larger than 5% of the playfield.
6. **G-zone-seams** (art, WS4, severity 3, effort S, about +0.5). Hard horizontal mask edges (`desktop/c01-row` y≈285 and y≈600). Fix: feather the masks 40–80 px along the guide geometry, or edge them with gold pinstripe.
7. **G-glare-halos** (lighting, WS1, severity 3, effort S, about +0.5). Blue glare (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/c06-arlington`) and halos that bury labels (`desktop/c01-row`). Fix: shrink halo radius by about 40% and clamp or redirect the key-light specular on the rail. Test: labels under lit inserts stay legible.
8. **G-mode-lighting** (lighting, WS1, severity 3, effort M, about +0.5). No mood shift in `desktop/r07-mode` or `desktop/r08-wizard`. Fix: tint GI per mode, and for the wizard drop GI to about 40% with chases and scoop flashers.
9. **G-dmd-copy** (ui, WS5, severity 3, effort S, about +0.5). Repeated text in `desktop/r08-wizard`, three-line cramming in `desktop/r06-multiball`, a static mode in `desktop/r07-mode`, no callouts in `desktop/r05-ramp`. Fix: two lines maximum with scrolling for long facts, rewrite the wizard copy, run the derby race animation in-mode, and add ramp and bumper callouts.
10. **G-playfield-lettering** (typography, WS4, severity 3, effort S, about +0.5). Unreadable mode labels, clipped SUPERIOR, hidden VAPORS, odd bumper glyph (`desktop/r03-play`, `desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c02-springs`). Fix: label cap height ≥2.5% of table width, move SUPERIOR clear of the scoop, letter VAPORS in front of the scoop, and use a clean face on the caps.
11. **G-mobile-launch-score** (mobile, WS6, severity 3, effort S, about +0.5). Launch covers the shooter lane, and the score vanishes during modes (`mobile/r06-multiball`, `mobile/r07-mode`). Fix: move Launch left of the shooter lane into the apron (or make the lane itself the touch target), and keep a score line in the DMD frame.
12. **G-painting-authenticity** (authenticity, WS4, severity 3, effort L, about +0.5). Pamukkale-style terraces and a palm-lined promenade (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/r03-play`). Fix: repaint the top zone as wooded Hot Springs Mountain with the tufa Hot Water Cascade and steaming springs, and swap the backglass palms for oaks and magnolias.
13. **G-event-feedback** (animation, WS3, severity 3, effort M, about +0.5). No sling kick or camera reaction in `strips/s01-flip`. Fix: sling plastic flash and rubber flex, plus a small camera shake on bumper hits and nudges.
14. **G-help-overflow** (ui, WS5, severity 2, effort S, about +0.25). Fix: a sticky footer with Back, and a scrolling body capped at 90vh (`desktop/r09-help`, `mobile/r09-help`).
15. **G-steam-sprites** (animation, WS3, severity 2, effort S, about +0.25). Fix: soft wisp textures that expand and fade, in place of the dot clusters (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`).
16. **G-skill-shot-lamp** (readability, WS1, severity 2, effort S, about +0.25). Fix: flash the lit S·P·A lane at about 2 Hz with a larger halo and an arrow (`desktop/r02-serve`).
17. **G-ui-font** (typography, WS5, severity 2, effort S, about +0.25). Fix: replace the system sans in menus with a deco-compatible UI face (`desktop/r09-help`).
