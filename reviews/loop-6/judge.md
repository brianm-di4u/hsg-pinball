# Judge: loop 6

## Ruling in brief

Final total **59.5 / 90**. Verdict: **continue**. Only UI (7.0) and authenticity (7.5) meet their gates. Every other
category sits at 6.0 to 6.5.

This loop's hardware kit is real progress at close range: the continuous clear ramp ribbon, the printed sling
plastics and the thin stainless guides. It doesn't lift any category over a gate on its own, because three things
are untouched:
- Nothing casts a shadow on the playfield.
- The glare faults remain.
- The play camera shrinks the table's detail.

The phone landscape framing went backwards.

The critics are within 1.0 of each other in every category. The prosecutor and advocate are exactly 1.0 apart in
art, lighting, UI, readability and mobile. None of these gaps is strictly over 1.0, but I opened the full-size
files, plus 4x crops of the points in dispute, before ruling. All paths are relative to
`games/Pinball/screenshots/review-loop-6/`.

### Factual disputes settled from the captures
- **Ball size.** In a 4x crop of `desktop/r03-play`, the ball measures about **16 px** across, rim included.
  - Critic A's "about 10 px" is wrong.
  - The advocate's 18 to 20 px is slightly generous.
  - Critic A's test for G-desktop-table-scale ("ball at least 14 px") is already met, so that gap is dropped.
- **Ball highlight.** The prosecutor is right. The ball's highlight is a "+"-shaped studio-panel cross, not a
  reflection of the table (`desktop/r03-play` crop, `strips/s01-flip`).
- **Bumper hotspots as ball decoys.** The prosecutor is right, though they are a little smaller than claimed.
  - Each pop bumper has a pure-white base hotspot about 8 to 10 px across.
  - Its brightness matches the ball's highlight (`desktop/r03-play` crop, `desktop/r06-multiball`).
  - In `desktop/r06-multiball`, with one ball at the arch, the upper field shows four ball-like white dots.
- **"MOUNTAIN TOW".** The advocate has the right cause. In a 4x crop of `mobile/r03-play`, the ramp plate continues
  past "TOW" and the COASTER legend plate sits over its end. The plate's length isn't the problem.
  `desktop/c03-flippers` is cropped by the frame edge, so it isn't evidence.
- **Score shown twice.** Critic A is wrong and the advocate is right.
  - `desktop/r03-play` shows the score only in the DMD.
  - `desktop/r04-bumpers` and `desktop/r07-mode` show it only in the side panel.
  - `desktop/r07-mode` also has a "Derby · 18/100 · 45s" progress and timer line, which Critic A said was missing.
- **Landscape A/B.** The prosecutor is right and Critic B read the pair backwards.
  - In `ab/mobile-r11-landscape`, A has the same narrow, flatter table as the standalone `mobile/r11-landscape`.
  - A's table is about 150 px wide in the pair, against about 210 px for B, at roughly the same height.
  - This loop's flatter camera made the landscape table smaller.
  - The advocate's "about 85% of the height" holds (y 115 to 780 of 780), but the width use is poor.
- **Bumper strip.** The prosecutor is mostly right about `strips/s02-bumpers`.
  - Frames 0 and 1 have the ball between the bumpers under a warm cap glow.
  - The teal body flashes come in frames 5 to 7, after the ball has left the top of the frame.
  - The 6 mm sink is at best faint.
- **Light show.** The advocate is right about `strips/s03-multiball`. An amber flasher washes the top right in frames
  0, 1, 2 and 7, and the lamp chase runs. In frames 4 to 7 the Valley star blooms into a white disc over its own
  legend.
- **The c04 flare.** It is real and large: the top-left quarter of `desktop/c04-vapors` is veiled white.

## Category rulings

### Art: 6.5 (critics 6.5 / 7.0, prosecutor 6.0, advocate 7.0)
- **Strengths:**
  - The backglass is excellent and in the same palette as the playfield (`desktop/r02-serve`).
  - The lower deco fan frames the flippers, with the multipliers on its spokes (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
  - The new sling print ties into the fan.
- **Mid-field.** The advocate is right that it isn't empty. The mode arc, arrows and legends fill the band below
  SPA CITY (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **What holds it below 7:**
  - The upper garden painting runs under the bumpers and the S·P·A guides without being composed around them
    (`desktop/c02-springs`, `strips/s02-bumpers`).
  - The shot arrows are flat outlined triangles (`desktop/c04-vapors`, foreground), not lensed inserts integrated
    into the art.
- **Score.** "Every zone themed" is met. "Inserts and lettering integrated" and "composed around the layout" are only
  half met. Critic B's 7 gives the backglass credit that belongs to the playfield.

### Toys: 6.5 (critics 6.5 / 6.5, prosecutor 6.0, advocate 6.5)
- **Improvements:**
  - The ramp ribbon with rounded lips reads as moulded plastic (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - The slings have printed bevelled plastics on chrome posts (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
  - The Arlington, the Quapaw dome and the tower read at a glance (`desktop/c01-row`).
- **Problems I confirmed:**
  - The ramp mouth still has two walnut boxes, which the clear lips end in points against, plus a seam in the bed
    (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - The Vapors scoop walls are flat grey slabs (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
  - The flippers are featureless white extrusions (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
  - All eight bathhouses sit on identical beige plinths (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The ball's cross highlight weakens the "chrome ball" requirement (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **Bumpers.** The advocate is right that the caps carry a gold ring and a printed 143°F decal (`desktop/c01-row`).
- **Score.** Still below the anchor's "every toy plausible".

### Lighting: 6.5 (critics 6.0 / 6.5, prosecutor 5.5, advocate 6.5)
- **For:**
  - Lamps bloom only when lit.
  - The bathhouse inserts no longer clip, so their plates stay legible (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The wizard mode dims the room and its environment for a real mood shift (`desktop/r08-wizard`).
  - The Grand Soak strip runs a flasher and lamp chase (`strips/s03-multiball`).
- **Against:**
  - No contact or cast shadow under any toy, flipper, plinth or the ball (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c03-flippers`).
  - A large blown flare (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
  - The Valley star becomes a white disc (`desktop/r08-wizard`, `strips/s03-multiball` frames 4 to 7).
  - Pure-white bumper base hotspots.
  - The ball shows a studio cross instead of the table.
- **Score.** That fails the 7 anchor's conditions on grounding, ball reflection and glare. The mode-driven mood
  shifts keep it above the prosecutor's 5.5.

### Animation: 6.5 (critics 6.5 / 6.5, prosecutor 6.0, advocate 6.5)
- **Shown:**
  - The flipper snaps in one frame and holds (`strips/s01-flip`).
  - The light show runs, with a flasher (`strips/s03-multiball`).
  - Steam rises from the springs.
- **Not shown:**
  - Bumper hit and reaction never appear together. The flashes come after the ball has left (`strips/s02-bumpers`,
    frames 5 to 7).
  - The sink is barely visible.
  - No camera reaction appears.
  - In `strips/s01-flip`, no lamp or sling reacts across the 8 frames.
- **Score.** Two of the 7 anchor's five items are clearly shown. The critics' 6.5 stands, and part of the fix is a
  better-framed s02 capture.

### UI: 7.0 (critics 7.0 / 7.5, prosecutor 6.5, advocate 7.5)
- **Corrections to Critic A.** The duplicate-score and missing-timer complaints are false (`desktop/r03-play`,
  `desktop/r04-bumpers`, `desktop/r07-mode`).
- **Meets the anchor:**
  - The DMD has character, with mode icons (the horse in `desktop/r07-mode`, the Arlington towers in
    `desktop/r06-multiball`).
  - The menus share one deco style (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r10-scores`).
  - The phone chip has its own row (`mobile/r03-play`).
- **What keeps it from 7.5:**
  - The desktop side panel is empty below Menu (`desktop/r03-play`).
  - The DMD idles on "SPA CITY / BALL 1" in `desktop/r04-bumpers`, and no DMD animation is evidenced.
  - The help screen's left column runs under Back mid-sentence while the right column is about 40% empty
    (`desktop/r09-help`).
  - The phone Menu and Launch buttons cover the apron cards (`mobile/r03-play`).
- **Score.** Meets the gate, not more.

### Typography: 6.5 (critics 6.5 / 6.5, prosecutor 6.0, advocate 6.5)
- **For:** a deliberate slab display face and UI sans (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r09-help`), a clean DMD font,
  and plates that are crisp in close-up (`desktop/c01-row`).
- **Against:**
  - At play scale the plates and apron cards are illegible (`desktop/r02-serve`, `mobile/r03-play`).
  - The ramp legend is truncated by the overlapping COASTER plate (`mobile/r03-play`).
  - Star bloom eats the VALLEY OF THE VAPORS legend (`desktop/r08-wizard`).
- **Score.** The 7 anchor's "playfield lettering crisp" fails in play frames.

### Readability: 6.5 (critics 6.0 / 6.5, prosecutor 5.5, advocate 6.5)
- **For:**
  - With the ball size corrected to about 16 px (`desktop/r03-play`), the ball reads well in the lower field.
  - It is visible on the ramp (`desktop/r05-ramp`).
  - Lit arrows clearly mark the shots (`desktop/r07-mode`, `desktop/r08-wizard`).
  - Lit and unlit mode inserts differ on both viewports (`mobile/r03-play`).
- **Against:**
  - The white bumper hotspots are genuine ball decoys in the upper field (`desktop/r06-multiball`).
  - Multiball is findable but not instant.
- **Score.** Critic A's 6.0 rested partly on the wrong ball size; the prosecutor's 5.5 overweights the decoys.

### Mobile: 6.0 (critics 6.0 / 6.0, prosecutor 5.5, advocate 6.5)
- **Portrait is framed on purpose** (`mobile/r02-serve`, `mobile/r03-play`):
  - The DMD is large.
  - The chip has its own row.
  - The table fills the width.
  - The buttons stay off the flippers and lanes.
- **Landscape fails the anchor:**
  - The table is small, with a large empty region to its left and a mostly empty HUD column (`mobile/r11-landscape`).
  - The table is smaller than last loop's (`ab/mobile-r11-landscape`; see the A/B dispute above).
  - No Launch control is visible.
- **Score.** One good orientation and one weaker than before averages to 6.0, not the advocate's 6.5.

### Authenticity: 7.5 (critics 7.5 / 7.5, prosecutor 7.0, advocate 7.5)
- **For:**
  - All eight bathhouses are labelled in true north-to-south order, with distinct silhouettes: the Quapaw dome and
    the Ozark twin towers (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The twin-towered Arlington (`desktop/c06-arlington`) and the lattice Mountain Tower (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - The help copy is hedged and the 1832 date is correct (`desktop/r09-help`).
- **Against.** The prosecutor's point about the Arlington is fair but minor. It sits centred in the Row as the lock,
  not at the north end, and the help text doesn't hedge this. That's a layout liberty, not an error a hedge line
  can't cover.
- **Score.** Gate met comfortably.

## IP ruling
**Clean.**
- No reviewer found a commercial pinball name, logo or art, and I saw none.
- The backglass contains no text (`desktop/r02-serve`). The horse-head shape in the steam is a painting artefact, not
  text or a logo.
- **The Vapors sign.** Critic B's `clean: false` rests only on an unverified question: whether the "The VAPORS" neon
  script in `desktop/c04-vapors` copies the real venue's sign.
  - The sign is set in the game's own faces: a thin script "The" and a block sans "VAPORS".
  - Nothing in the captures shows reproduced logo lettering.
  - The measured IP gate passed.
  - This is not a hit and no severity-5 gap is raised. The developer may record a source comparison in
    `art-src/PROMPTS.md`.

     - Cap the Valley star's emissive below the bloom threshold.
     - Cut the specular on the bumper caps.
   - **Test:**
     - No saturated white area larger than 40 px in c04, r08 or s03 frames 4 to 7.
     - "143°F" legible on every cap in every s02 frame.

   - **Fix:** replace the white bumper-base point sprites with a dim teal ring glow on the skirt.
   - **Test:** in r02 and r06, no non-ball blob with luminance above 0.9 is larger than half the ball's diameter.
4. **G-bumper-hit-sync** (animation, S, WS3, +0.4)
   - **Fix:**
     - Peak the bumper flash and the 6 mm sink on the contact frame, with a 2 to 3 frame decay.
     - Add a sling-rubber flash and a brief GI pulse on bumper hits.
     - Re-frame the s02 capture on the bumpers for the whole hit and recovery.
   - **Test:** one s02 frame shows the ball touching a visibly sunk, flashing bumper.
5. **G-playfield-legend-overlap** (typography, S, WS4, +0.3)
   - **Fix:**
     - Move the COASTER legend off the end of the ramp plate so MOUNTAIN TOWER shows whole.
     - Set a minimum plate cap height of about 7 px at 1280×800 and about 8 device px at 390 px.
     - Cut the apron card body text to one large line.
   - **Test:** "MOUNTAIN TOWER" is fully legible in `desktop/r02-serve` and `mobile/r03-play`.
6. **G-ramp-entrance** (toys, S, WS2, +0.3)
   - **Fix:**
     - Replace the two walnut boxes at the ramp mouth with stainless blade posts.
     - End each clear lip in a rounded return.
     - Add a thin metal entrance flap that meets the playfield.
     - Remove the seam in the ramp bed.
   - **Test:** c05 shows no brown box, no pointed lip end and no bed seam.
7. **G-dmd-and-side-panel** (ui, S, WS5, +0.3)
   - **Fix:**
     - Keep the score and one live objective line on the DMD in every play state, instead of idling on
       "SPA CITY / BALL 1".
     - Give each mode icon a 2 to 4 frame cycle.
     - Fill the empty space under Menu with a mode-progress lamp grid.
     - On the help screen, balance the columns so nothing runs under Back.
   - **Test:**
     - Every desktop play frame's DMD shows the score and an objective.
     - No help text is cut by the Back button at first view.
8. **G-grounding-shadows** (lighting, **L, the big lever**, WS1, +0.75)
   - **Problem:** no contact or cast shadow under any toy, flipper, plinth or the ball. This is the one condition of
     the 7 anchor that every reviewer conceded is missing. It also holds back toys, which float, and readability.
   - **Fix:**
     - A soft shadow-mapped key light (PCF or VSM) from the GI direction for the bathhouses, tower, Arlington,
       slings, flippers, posts and ramp.
     - Baked AO and contact decals under each plinth and post.
     - A soft blob shadow under every ball.
     - Keep the Low tier on the baked decals only, within the perf gates.
   - **Test:**
     - Visible darkening on the art under every bathhouse in c01 and under the flippers in c03.
     - A dark contact spot under the ball in r03.
     - Perf gates still pass.
9. **G-ball-env-reflection** (lighting, M, WS1, +0.4)
   - **Fix:** give the ball an environment map rendered from the playfield (a low-res CubeCamera updated every few
     frames, or a baked playfield cubemap), with a slightly darker fresnel rim.
   - **Test:** at 4x, the ball in r03 shows warm playfield colour and no "+" cross.
10. **G-grey-slab-walls** (toys, M, WS2, +0.4)
    - **Fix:** rebuild the Vapors scoop walls and the Arlington lock back wall in the new brushed-stainless blade
      profile, with a rolled top and visible screw heads.
    - **Test:** c04 and c06 show no flat, untextured grey faces.
11. **G-lensed-inserts** (art, M, WS4, +0.4)
    - **Fix:**
      - Model the shot arrows and the star as bevelled, lensed inserts set into the playfield and lit from below.
      - Darken and simplify the printed art immediately around each upper-field entrance so the lit arrow is the
        only cue there.
    - **Test:** in c04 the arrows show a bevel and an inner lens gradient.
12. **G-flipper-plinth-detail** (toys, M, WS2, +0.3)
    - **Fix:**
      - Add a deco decal, a bevelled edge and a rubber texture to the flippers.
      - Give each bathhouse plinth bevelled edges and a stone or brick texture that differs per building.
    - **Test:** c03 flippers show a decal and a bevel. No two c01 plinths are identical plain beige cubes.
