# Critic A (critic-claude): loop 6

## Verdict

Spa City Pinball is a competent, coherent browser table. It still isn't a shipped digital pinball table. The close-ups hold up better than before. The ramp is now a continuous clear ribbon (`desktop/c05-tower`), the slingshots carry printed plastics on chrome posts (`desktop/c03-flippers`), and the eight bathhouses read as distinct buildings in the correct order (`desktop/c01-row`). Seen through the play camera, though, the table is a 460 px column on a 1280 px screen. The ball is about 10 px of silver on light terrazzo, and almost every plate and apron card turns to mush (`desktop/r02-serve`, `desktop/r03-play`). Several walls still read as flat grey or brown slabs (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`, the ramp entrance in `desktop/c05-tower`). Toys cast almost no visible grounding shadow. There are clear glare and bloom faults: a blown white flare in the top-left of `desktop/c04-vapors`, and the "143°F" bumper caps erased by hotspots in `desktop/s02-bumpers-5`. Phone landscape wastes most of the screen (`mobile/r11-landscape`). Authenticity is the strongest category and the DMD and menus are solid. Everything visual sits in the 6 to 7 band, and the readability and mobile gates are the furthest from passing. Two capture-quality notes: `desktop/r04-bumpers` and `mobile/r04-bumpers` show the ball at the top arch, not in the pop bumpers, so bumper action can only be judged from `strips/s02-bumpers`.

## Scores

### Art: 6.5
- The backglass is the best single asset (`desktop/r02-serve`, left panel). It's a painted night view of the Row with a domed bathhouse, a twin-towered hotel, a lattice tower, a neon canopy and magnolias, and the backglass and playfield art share a palette.
- The lower playfield is designed around the layout. A terrazzo deco fan radiates from the flippers, and the 2X to 5X inserts sit on its spokes (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
- The upper third is a dense painted garden, spring house and waterfall that the bumpers and S·P·A lanes simply sit on top of (`desktop/c02-springs`). The painting doesn't direct the eye to any shot.
- The middle of the field, between the SPA CITY logo and the slingshots, is a large plain brown terrazzo patch with a few flat triangle decals (`desktop/r03-play`, `mobile/r03-play`). The arrows are flat printed shapes, not lensed inserts.
- The slingshot plastics are an improvement. The deco sunrise and steam-wisp print (`desktop/c03-flippers`) beats the plain fills shown on the other side of `ab/desktop-c03-flippers`.

### Toys: 6.5
- **Reads well:**
  - The Arlington: twin towers, red roofs and recessed lit windows (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - The Quapaw dome mosaic and the Fordyce skylight roof (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c01-row`).
  - The Mountain Tower: a lattice mast with an octagonal lit cab (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - The chrome wireform with cross-ties, and the chrome ball (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
- **Ramp:** the new clear ribbon walls with rounded lips read as moulded plastic (`desktop/c05-tower`, `ab/desktop-c05-tower`). The ramp floor still shows a hard seam halfway up, and the entrance is flanked by plain dark-brown boxes.
- **Grey blocks remain:**
  - The Vapors scoop walls are flat grey extruded slabs on a beige box (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
  - The Arlington lock frame is a flat grey U with a glowing top edge (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - The bathhouses sit on plain beige plinths.
- **Flippers and bumpers lack detail.** Flippers are plain white extrusions with a red rubber band, with no decal and no wear (`desktop/c03-flippers`). Bumper bodies are plain teal cylinders with a chrome base and no ring or skirt detail (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Scale:** close-ups only partly reach the "machine photographed" level, and at the play camera most toys shrink to a few pixels (`desktop/r03-play`).

### Lighting: 6.0
- **What works:**
  - Lamps bloom only when lit, and lit inserts are clearly different from unlit ones (`desktop/c03-flippers`, 2X and 3X against 4X and 5X).
  - GI warms the lower field.
  - Wizard mode dims the room and the environment (`desktop/r08-wizard`, `mobile/r08-wizard`), which is a real mood shift.
  - Lit bathhouse inserts no longer clip, so their plates stay legible (`desktop/c01-row`).
- **Glare and blown areas:**
  - `desktop/c04-vapors` has a large blown white flare in the top-left corner.
  - In `desktop/s02-bumpers-5` the specular hotspots on two bumper caps erase the "143°F" lettering.
  - In `desktop/r08-wizard` the Valley star becomes a white bloom blob that swallows its legend.
  - The bumper base point-lights are hard white dots (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
- **Grounding:** visible contact shadows are weak or absent under the bathhouses, the tower and the slingshots (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c05-tower`). Toys look placed on the art rather than lit with it.
- **Room:** outside the cabinet the room is a pure black void (`desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/s03-multiball-4`).

### Animation: 6.5
- `strips/s01-flip`: the ball drops onto the left flipper (frames 0–2), the flipper snaps up in one frame (frame 3), and the ball travels up the field (frames 4–7). The snap reads, but there's no anticipation, follow-through or table reaction.
- `strips/s02-bumpers`: bumper bodies flash teal on hits (frames 5–7) and steam wisps rise from the springs. The new 6 mm sink isn't readable at strip scale, and the steam is soft white blobs.
- `strips/s03-multiball`: the insert light show steps between frames (purple mode arc, multiplier arrows, lock lamps), and the room darkens around it. Good, but no GI sweep or flasher burst is visible.
- No camera reaction to nudges or hits appears in any capture.

### UI: 7.0
- The DMD has character: pixel icons per mode (the Arlington towers in `desktop/r06-multiball`, the horse in `desktop/r07-mode` and `mobile/r07-mode`, the steaming mountain in `desktop/r08-wizard`) and clean dot-matrix text.
- Menus share one deco style: gold frame, purple buttons, gold primary (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r10-scores`).
- On phone the progress chip now has its own row under the DMD instead of sitting over the cabinet (`mobile/r02-serve`).
- **Weak spots:**
  - On desktop the right panel is mostly dead space under the Menu button (`desktop/r03-play`).
  - The score is shown twice, in the DMD and in the side panel (`desktop/r04-bumpers`).
  - The DMD is a single static orange frame per mode: the captures show no animation, progress bar or timer graphics.

### Typography: 6.5
- The display face (a Clarendon-style slab, neon-lit on the title) and the UI sans are deliberate and paired well (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r09-help`, `mobile/r09-help`).
- The DMD dot font is clean (`mobile/r06-multiball`).
- Playfield lettering is crisp in close-ups (`desktop/c01-row`: SUPERIOR, HALE, QUAPAW and so on), but illegible at the play camera: bathhouse plates, THE ARLINGTON and both apron cards are unreadable in `desktop/r02-serve` and `mobile/r02-serve`.
- The ramp plate reads "MOUNTAIN TOW" because the text is clipped by its plate (`desktop/r02-serve`, `desktop/c03-flippers`, `mobile/r02-serve`, `desktop/s03-multiball-4`).
- The Vapors marquee is a white neon smear at play scale (`desktop/r02-serve`).

### Readability: 6.0
- On desktop the whole table is about 460 px wide on a 1280×800 screen and the ball is about 10 px across. It's silver on light tan terrazzo, so it holds up in the lower field (`desktop/r03-play`) but is easy to lose against the bright garden art and steam near the top (`desktop/r04-bumpers`, ball at the arch).
- In multiball (`desktop/r06-multiball`) the three balls are tiny chrome dots: one at the arch, one at the flipper tips, one in the plunger lane. They're findable, but not an instant read.
- Shot entrances are signalled by coloured arrows (`desktop/r07-mode`, where lit arrows glow clearly). Lit and unlit purple mode inserts are clear on both viewports (`mobile/r03-play`).
- On phone the ball reads better because the table is larger (`mobile/r03-play`, `mobile/r05-ramp`).

### Mobile: 6.0
- **Portrait is framed on purpose:**
  - The DMD at the top is large and readable.
  - The chip sits on its own row, and the table fills the width (`mobile/r02-serve`, `mobile/r06-multiball`).
  - The Menu and Launch buttons sit over the apron, not the ball's path, though they hide the apron cards.
- **Landscape fails the "framed on purpose" bar.** In `mobile/r11-landscape` the table takes roughly 40% of the 844 px width, has about 115 px of black above it, and leaves wide black bands on both sides. Next to the other build in `ab/mobile-r11-landscape`, this build's flatter camera makes the table visibly narrower and smaller.

### Authenticity: 7.5
- All eight bathhouses are present and labelled in true north-to-south order (Superior, Hale, Maurice, Fordyce, Quapaw, Ozark, Buckstaff, Lamar) in `desktop/c01-row`. Their silhouettes differ in plausible ways: the Quapaw dome, the Ozark twin towers, the Fordyce skylight.
- The landmarks are all there: the twin-towered Arlington (`desktop/c06-arlington`), the lattice Mountain Tower (`desktop/c05-tower`), the Vapors neon marquee (`desktop/c04-vapors`), and steaming thermal springs at 143 °F (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- Central Avenue and Reserve St are lettered on the field (`mobile/r02-serve`).
- The help text hedges properly ("about" or "reportedly", fictional horses, no affiliation) and states a correct fact: the reservation was created on April 20, 1832, forty years before Yellowstone (`desktop/r09-help`).
- Minus: the bathhouse models are toy-block simplifications on beige plinths, and the backglass dome reads as generic Mediterranean.

## IP check

- The captures show no commercial pinball machine name, logo or art anywhere.
- The backglass painting has no legible text, including the neon canopy at the right edge, which is blank (checked at 2× crop of `desktop/r02-serve`). There is a faint horse-head shape in the steam at the upper right of the backglass. It isn't text or a logo, but it reads as an accidental artefact.
- Real landmark names (the Arlington, the Vapors, the bathhouses) appear as set dressing in the game's own type. The "The VAPORS" neon script on the scoop sign (`desktop/c04-vapors`) should be checked by eye against the real venue's sign, so it doesn't copy its logo lettering. I couldn't verify that from the captures, and it isn't counted as a hit.
- Verdict: clean.

   - **Fix:** widen the table viewport to at least 55% of the screen width by narrowing the backglass panel, or add a gentle ball-follow zoom.
   - **Test:** the ball is at least 14 px in r03.

   - **Problem:** the Vapors scoop walls and the Arlington lock frame are flat grey slabs, and the ramp entrance has brown boxes (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c05-tower`).

   - **Test:** c04 and c06 show no flat untextured grey faces.
4. **G-glare-bloom-clip** (lighting, severity 3, effort S, WS1, about +0.5)
   - **Problem:** a blown flare at the top-left of c04, hotspots erasing the 143°F caps in `desktop/s02-bumpers-5`, and a white bloom blob over the Valley star in `desktop/r08-wizard`.
   - **Fix:** clamp the bloom input and lower the specular intensity on bumper caps. Find the light causing the c04 flare and pull it back. Cap the emissive of the Valley star insert.
   - **Test:** 143°F stays legible in every s02 frame, and no saturated white area larger than 40 px appears in c04 or r08.
5. **G-contact-shadows** (lighting, severity 3, effort M, WS1, about +0.5)
   - **Problem:** the bathhouses, tower and slingshots aren't grounded by shadows (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/c03-flippers`).
   - **Fix:** turn on soft shadow casting for toys, or bake AO and contact-shadow decals under each toy and plinth.
   - **Test:** visible darkening on the art directly under each bathhouse in c01.
6. **G-playfield-text-scale** (typography, severity 3, effort S, WS4, about +0.5)

   - **Fix:** widen the ramp plate so the whole word fits. Set a minimum cap height of about 7 px for plates at 1280×800, and drop or enlarge the apron card body text.
   - **Test:** "MOUNTAIN TOWER" is fully visible in r02.
7. **G-ball-contrast** (readability, severity 3, effort S, WS1, about +0.4)
   - **Problem:** the silver ball blends into the light terrazzo and the bright garden art (`desktop/r04-bumpers`, `desktop/r06-multiball`).
   - **Fix:** add a darker fresnel rim and a contact shadow under the ball, and keep its reflections a step darker than the field.
   - **Test:** local luminance contrast of at least 3:1 in r04 and r06.
8. **G-animation-legibility** (animation, severity 3, effort M, WS3, about +0.5)
   - **Problem:** the bumper sink, any flipper follow-through and any camera reaction aren't visible in the strips (`strips/s01-flip`, `strips/s02-bumpers`).
   - **Fix:** add a bright bumper ring and skirt flash with a visible drop, a short flipper-hit flash on the slingshot rubber, a GI pulse on bumper hits, and a small camera shake on slings and nudges.
   - **Test:** at least two s02 frames show a visibly depressed, flashing bumper ring.

    - **Problem:** plain white flippers and plain teal bumper cylinders (`desktop/c03-flippers`, `desktop/c02-springs`).
    - **Fix:** add a deco flipper decal and rubber texture, plus a bumper metal ring, skirt and cap insert ring.
11. **G-desktop-side-panel** (ui, severity 2, effort S, WS5, about +0.3)
    - **Problem:** dead space under Menu, and the score is shown twice (`desktop/r03-play`, `desktop/r04-bumpers`).
