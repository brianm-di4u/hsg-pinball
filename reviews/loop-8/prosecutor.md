# Prosecutor: loop 8

## Summary

The critic means are art 7.0, toys 6.25, lighting 6.5, animation 6.25, UI 6.75, typography 6.5, readability 6.5, mobile 6.5 and authenticity 7.25, a total of about 59.5. Both critics name the right weaknesses but score them too kindly in several places. Three findings carry most of the argument:
- **Literal anchor matches the critics excused.** In toys, the rubric's anchor-5 wording ("rails and walls read as grey blocks") describes `desktop/c04-vapors` and `desktop/c06-arlington` exactly.
- **Claimed changes the captures don't show.** The phone mode chip (`mobile/r07-mode`), the "whole field" multiball strip (`strips/s03-multiball`), the spinner fix (`mobile/r03-play`) and the desktop width (`desktop/r03-play`, about 460 px against the 576 px claimed).
- **Defects neither critic raised.**
  - The DMD idles on "SPA CITY / BALL 1" through the ramp and bumper frames.
  - The Mountain Tower ramp mouth has no shot lamp, even in the "ALL SHOTS LIT" wizard.
  - Painted lantern posts are as tall as the 3D bathhouses.
  - Wireform supports end in mid-air.
  - The menus, HUD, help and scores use a system sans.

Proposed total: **55.0 / 90**.

I opened every full-size frame, all six close-ups and all three strips, plus 2× crops of the `desktop/r03-play` backglass and lower plaza, the `desktop/r08-wizard` upper field, the `desktop/c06-arlington` hotel and the `desktop/c05-tower` tower base.

## Category by category

### Art: critics 6.5 / 7.5 (mean 7.0). Proposed **6.5**
Critic B's 7.5 claims the art is past the 7 anchor ("painted art composed around the layout, every zone themed, inserts and lettering integrated"). The captures don't support that.
- **Inserts are stickers on the art, not integrated.** The bathhouse inserts are olive rectangles and the mode inserts are purple discs. Neither has a painted surround, halo or bezel (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/r03-play` lower-plaza crop). The 2X–5X triangles float on the terrazzo fan (`desktop/c03-flippers`). The "MOUNTAIN TOWER" plate is laid at an angle across the fan with no relation to the painting (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
- **Painted props fight the models' scale.** The lantern posts painted along the promenade are as tall as or taller than the 3D bathhouses beside them: the left and right lanterns in `desktop/c06-arlington`, and the lanterns behind Fordyce and Quapaw in `desktop/c01-row`. That makes the Row look like dollhouses parked in front of giant street lamps. Neither critic noticed this.
- **Texel softness.** At close range the water and cobble painting under the bumpers is visibly smeared and upscaled next to the crisp models (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/c06-arlington`). Critic B noted this but still scored 7.5.
- **Lower third.** It is a flat graphic fan over mottled terrazzo, with empty speckled areas outside both slings and at the outlanes (`desktop/r03-play`, `desktop/c03-flippers`). That is anchor-5 "empty patches" in a third of the field.
- **What is good.** The upper repaint and the backglass are genuinely good (`desktop/c02-springs`, `desktop/r03-play` left panel). Half the table sits at the 7 anchor and half nearer 5.5, so 6.5 is the honest score.

### Toys: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed **5.5**
- **The anchor-5 wording fits.** The rubric's 5 is "recognisable models in flat materials; rails and walls read as grey blocks". That is what the close-ups show:
  - The Vapors scoop walls are two untextured grey slabs with no cap, trim or bevel. The sign is a flat billboard stood on a cream box (`desktop/c04-vapors`).
  - The Arlington lock frame is the same grey extrusion (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - A long opaque grey slab runs the whole left side of the ramp, and a grey block sits under the ramp entrance (`desktop/c05-tower`, left and bottom edges).
- **Flat bodies, blank bases.**
  - The bathhouses are boxes on featureless cream plinths. Sinking them into the field did not turn the plinths into stoops (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The Arlington's window texture breaks up: some windows are black, some half-lit with ragged dark marks (2× crop of `desktop/c06-arlington`, lower-left tower and right wing).
- **Hardware the critics didn't mention.**
  - The wireform's vertical supports stop in mid-air above the cabinet with no foot or bracket (`desktop/c05-tower`, crop at right).
  - The lattice tower stands on four plain cream cubes (`desktop/c05-tower`).
- **Flippers and slings.** The flippers are plain white bars with a red stripe. The slingshot "rubber" is a dark brown bar, not rings around posts (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
- **What reaches 7.** The bumpers (teal caps, chrome skirts), the lattice tower, the Quapaw dome and the chrome ball are at 7-level. They lift the score off 5, but not to 6.25.

### Lighting: critics 6.5 / 6.5 (mean 6.5). Proposed **6.0**
- **No grounding.** No toy has a contact shadow or AO:
  - The Arlington sits on the painted pool with no shadow at all (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c02-springs`).
  - The bathhouse plinths meet the brick with no darkening (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The 7 anchor requires "soft shadows ground every toy".
- **Glare is not solved.**
  - Hard white specular streaks show on a tower leg and on a wireform crossbar (`desktop/c05-tower`, crop).
  - The bumper caps carry a blue-white hot spot that sits on the "143°F" lettering (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c02-springs`).
  - The spinner still renders as a white bar in `mobile/r03-play` (left orbit, about y=770), although `mobile/r07-mode` shows the new painted flag. The fix depends on the angle.
- **The wizard dims; it doesn't run a show.** `desktop/r08-wizard` lowers the GI and turns on the existing inserts. Critic A's "real mood shift" overstates it: no colour wash, no flasher, nothing changes on the playfield art. Derby Day changes only inserts (`desktop/r07-mode`).
- **Bloom on the phone.** Bloom washes the multiplier and mode labels in `mobile/r08-wizard`, as critic B noted.
- **GI.** The lower field is flat, dim brown with no warm pools at the inlanes or slings (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **What works.** Lamps bloom only where lit, and the bumper hit pool is warm (`strips/s02-bumpers`).

### Animation: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed **6.0**
- **Flip strip.** In `strips/s01-flip` the flipper goes from rest to fully up between frames 2 and 3. It holds there through frame 7. Across all eight frames no lamp, sling or other object changes. That is a strike with no anticipation and no secondary reaction.
- **Bumper strip.** In `strips/s02-bumpers` the cap flashes and a warm pool appears (frames 1–2 and 5–6), but the skirt never drops. The steam columns barely change silhouette.
- **Multiball strip.** In `strips/s03-multiball` the table fills only about the left half of each panel; the right half is black. The arch and bumpers are clipped at the top and the flippers at the bottom (frames 0–7). The balls are 4–5 px specks. The insert chase is the only motion that reads. The brief's "frames the whole field" is not met.
- **Camera.** No camera reaction is visible in any strip or frame.
- **Verdict.** "Flippers snap, bumpers flash, light shows run" covers about half the 7 anchor, and the drop, the steam and the camera are missing. Critic B's 6.5 credits "changing steam" that the frames hardly show.

### UI: critics 6.5 / 7.0 (mean 6.75). Proposed **6.0**
- **The DMD doesn't react to events.** Neither critic caught this.
  - With the ball on the Mountain Tower ramp, both DMDs read "SPA CITY / BALL 1" (`desktop/r05-ramp`, `mobile/r05-ramp`).
  - In the bumper frames the DMD shows the same idle text (`desktop/r04-bumpers`, `mobile/r04-bumpers`).
  - The DMD has character in mode starts (`desktop/r07-mode`, `desktop/r06-multiball`), but its "readable animations" never follow play.
- **A claimed change is missing.** The phone chip shows only "1,274,560" during Derby Day (`mobile/r07-mode`). Both critics flagged this, yet critic B still gave 7.0.
- **HUD inconsistency.**
  - The score jumps between the DMD (`desktop/r03-play`) and the HUD line (`desktop/r04-bumpers`, `desktop/r05-ramp`).
  - The DMD's "BALL 1" second line is dim orange on brown and barely legible (`desktop/r03-play`, `mobile/r11-landscape`).
- **Not deco.** The mode chips are flat rounded rectangles in a system sans (`desktop/r03-play`). About 200 px of dead space sits above the DMD and again below Menu (`desktop/r03-play`).

### Typography: critics 6.5 / 6.5 (mean 6.5). Proposed **6.0**
- **There is no deliberate UI face.** The 7 anchor asks for "deliberate display and UI faces". The display slab of the title and headers is deliberate (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r09-help`). Everything else uses what looks like the platform's default sans:
  - the Play, How to play, High scores and Settings buttons (`desktop/r01-title`, `mobile/r01-title`);
  - the HUD, mode chips and key hints (`desktop/r03-play`);
  - the help body and the high-score initials (`desktop/r10-scores`, `mobile/r10-scores`).
- **Playfield lettering is unreadable at play scale.** Lane and insert labels are about 5 px on desktop and smaller on the phone (`desktop/r03-play`, `mobile/r03-play`). The "S·P·A" lane letters, which carry the skill shot, are a few pixels high (`desktop/r02-serve`).
- **Low-contrast copy.** The About copy in help is small grey on plum (`desktop/r09-help`).
- **What works.** The DMD dot font is clean (`mobile/r03-play`).

### Readability: critics 6.5 / 6.5 (mean 6.5). Proposed **6.0**
- **The ramp has no shot lamp.** There is no arrow or insert at the Mountain Tower ramp entrance in any frame (`desktop/r03-play`). Its only label, "MOUNTAIN TOWER", sits by the right inlane, far from the mouth. Even in the wizard, whose DMD reads "ALL SHOTS LIT", the ramp mouth is dark while the orbits, lock and inserts glow (`desktop/r08-wizard`, crop). The table's signature shot has no lamp language. Critic B touched this ("tower arrow displaced") but didn't price it.
- **The skill shot is a speck.** In `desktop/r02-serve` the DMD says "SKILL SHOT: LANE A". On the table the cue is one small gold lamp at the top right, beside lane letters about 5 px high.
- **The ball gets lost.**
  - In `desktop/r05-ramp` the ball is a pale ~10 px dot on clear plastic between silver wireform tubes. It is findable only if you know where to look.
  - In `desktop/r04-bumpers` the ball sits in the top arch above the bumpers, against bright steam.
  - In `desktop/r06-multiball` the three balls are ~12 px dots.
- **What works.** Lit and unlit inserts are clearly different (`desktop/r07-mode`), and portrait reads better (`mobile/r06-multiball`).

### Mobile: critics 6.5 / 6.5 (mean 6.5). Proposed **6.0**
- **Portrait is framed on purpose.** The buttons sit on the apron, clear of the flippers (`mobile/r03-play`, `mobile/r06-multiball`).
- **Landscape misses the 7 anchor.** The 7 anchor asks for a table "framed on purpose in portrait **and** landscape" with a readable DMD. In `mobile/r11-landscape`:
  - The table spans about 510 of 1688 px, roughly half its own centre column, with black margins on both sides.
  - The apron runs to the bottom edge of the frame.
  - The DMD is a small box in the corner whose "BALL 1" line is unreadable.
  - Playfield labels are illegible.
  - No touch-zone cue is shown.
- **Missing state.** The phone gives no mode progress or time (`mobile/r07-mode`).

### Authenticity: critics 7.0 / 7.5 (mean 7.25). Proposed **7.0**
- **Present and ordered.** The eight bathhouses are named in the right order (Superior to Lamar, `desktop/c01-row`). The tower, the Arlington, the Vapors and the steam are all present, and the help hedges facts with the correct 1832 date (`desktop/r09-help`). That meets the 7 anchor.
- **Why not 7.5.**
  - The Arlington model sits in the middle of the Row, between Fordyce and Quapaw (`desktop/c01-row`). The real hotel faces the north end, beyond Superior. The layout cuts the Row in half.
  - Several façades read only by their labels: Buckstaff is a plain white box with no colonnade, and Superior and Lamar are generic (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The "Display Spring" is painted as a broad wooded lake rather than the small tufa cascade (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Fair as stylisation, but not 9-direction.** Critic B's 7.5 isn't earned.

## IP check
Clean on the evidence.
- **Backglass.** At 2× (`desktop/r03-play` left panel) the painting has no letters in the neon canopy, the buildings or the street. "SPA CITY PINBALL" is applied type.
- **Playfield painting.** The upper painting has no text (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Trademarks.** Nothing resembles a commercial pinball title, logo or art.
- **The Vapors sign.** It is a script-plus-neon treatment of a real venue name (`desktop/c04-vapors`). I can't match it to a real logo, and it's used as a place label. It's worth keeping it visibly original.
