# Advocate: loop 8

Capture paths are relative to `games/Pinball/screenshots/review-loop-8/`. I opened every capture cited below at full size. Where I measured something, I cropped the PNG and say so.

## Summary

Most of what the critics say is right. The guide walls are grey slabs. The flippers are plain. Contact shadows are weak. The phone chip shows no mode state. The landscape table is small. The multiball strip does not frame the whole field.

The critics also make three factual errors, and each one pushes a score down:

1. **Ball size.** Critic A says the multiball balls in `desktop/r06-multiball` are "about 12 px dots". I cropped all three balls (at 608,43, 626,707 and 826,703) and each is about 17 px across. That already passes Critic A's own test for G-desktop-table-scale ("ball ≥ 16 px in r06").
2. **Bumper drop.** Critic A says the bumper skirt and ring "never visibly drop" in `strips/s02-bumpers`. Cropping the struck right-hand bumper shows the opposite. Its cap sits about 12–15 px lower in frames 1–3 than in frame 0, and the skirt under it changes from dark chrome to lit teal. Its height keeps changing through frames 4–7.
3. **Tower arrow.** Critic B says "the tower arrow is displaced from the ramp mouth". In `mobile/r05-ramp`, the yellow arrow at about (530,1115) points up and to the right, straight at the ramp's entry flap about 40 px away.

There are smaller overstatements too: "near-static" steam, "bloom swallowing multiplier labels", sling rubbers that are "not rubber rings", and "generic boxes" for the bathhouses.

My proposed total is **61.0 / 90**. The critics' means add up to 59.5.

## Category by category

Critic means are in brackets.

### Art: proposed 7.0 (A 6.5, B 7.5, mean 7.0)
- **Concede: the upper repaint and backglass are strong.** Both critics say so and the captures agree. In `desktop/c02-springs` and `desktop/r03-play` the bumpers sit on calm water with tufa and the cascade behind them. The backglass in `desktop/r03-play` is clearly by the same hand as the playfield.
- **Concede: the lower plaza is softer at close range.** `desktop/c03-flippers` shows upscaled softness in the terrazzo speckle. The inserts are plain plates without painted halos (`desktop/c01-row`, the olive rectangles for unlit bathhouses).
- **Rebut: the lower half is not "5.5-anchor".** The rubric's 5 anchor is "art sits in rectangles unrelated to the layout". The lower plaza does the opposite:
  - In `desktop/r03-play` and `mobile/r03-play`, the deco fan's rays converge on the Shoot Again star and the centre drain.
  - Its outer arcs run parallel to the arc of mode inserts (Soak to Coaster).
  - The 2X–5X triangles sit on the fan's centre petals.
  That is art composed around the layout, which is the 7-anchor wording. It is plainer than the upper third, but it is not unrelated to the layout.
- **Partly rebut: the "SPA CITY" seam.** In `desktop/r03-play` the banner sits exactly where the brick promenade meets the terrazzo plaza. The painting stages that as a paved terrace stepping down to the plaza, so it is a designed change of material, not a raw seam. It is still a visible change of rendering style, so this rebuttal is partial.
- **Score.** The mean of 7.0 is fair. Critic A's 6.5 rests on the "5.5 lower half" reading, which the captures don't support.

### Toys: proposed 6.5 (A 6.0, B 6.5, mean 6.25)
- **Concede: walls, flippers, plinths and the ramp flap.**
  - The scoop and lock walls are untextured grey slabs (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - The flippers are plain white bats with a red rubber and a chrome pivot cap (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
  - The bathhouses sit on cream plinths (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - A grey entry flap shows at the ramp mouth (bottom of `desktop/c05-tower`; also clear in a crop of `mobile/r05-ramp`).
- **Rebut: the slings, a factual overstatement by Critic A.** Critic A says the slingshot rubbers are "a dark brown bar, not rubber rings around posts". In `desktop/c03-flippers` each sling has:
  - a black rubber band along its inner face, which is how a sling rubber stretched between posts looks from above;
  - black rubber rings around white post bases at the corners, at about (265,320) and (1020,320).
  The posts on the scoop and lock walls also carry white rubber rings (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).
- **Rebut: "generic boxes".** `desktop/c01-row` shows eight different silhouettes and rooflines:
  - a green mansard;
  - a red gable;
  - the Fordyce's blue and gold skylight;
  - the Quapaw's tiled mosaic dome with its lantern;
  - the Ozark's twin red-capped towers;
  - Buckstaff's flat parapet;
  - Lamar's red trim.
  The Arlington has its twin towers and hipped roofs. The tower in `desktop/c05-tower` has a lattice frame, an octagonal glazed deck and a railing. The wireform has real cross-ties, and the ramp plastic is clear enough to show the art under it.
- **Score.** Against the 7 anchor ("every toy reads at a glance… convincing ramp and wireform, a chrome ball"), the reading part is met. The materials part is not. That puts the score above Critic A's 6.0 and at Critic B's 6.5.

### Lighting: proposed 6.5 (A 6.5, B 6.5, mean 6.5)
- **Concede.**
  - There is little contact shadow under the façades (`desktop/c01-row`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - Derby Day changes the inserts but not the mood (`desktop/r07-mode` against `desktop/r03-play`).
  - White specular streaks remain on the wireform cross-tie and a tower leg (`desktop/c05-tower`, lower right).
  - The spinner still reads as a white bar on the left orbit in `mobile/r03-play`, at about (70–125, 770), even though the brief says it was repainted.
- **Rebut: Critic B's "bloom swallowing multiplier labels" blames the wrong cause.** In `mobile/r08-wizard`, the 2X, 3X, 4X and 5X labels are still legible under the four lit triangles at about y=1353. In `desktop/r08-wizard` they are hard to read because they are tiny. They are just as tiny unlit in `desktop/r03-play`, so the cause is label size (typography), not bloom. The purple glow around the mode arc in `mobile/r08-wizard` is a soft halo about one insert wide, not a "broad haze".
- **Credit both critics gave fairly.**
  - Bloom appears only on lit lamps (`desktop/c03-flippers`: lit 2X and 3X against dull 4X and 5X).
  - The wizard drops the GI (`desktop/r08-wizard`).
  - A bumper hit throws a warm pool that decays (`strips/s02-bumpers`, frames 1, 2 and 6).
- **Score.** Hold at 6.5.

### Animation: proposed 6.5 (A 6.0, B 6.5, mean 6.25)
- **Rebut: "the skirt and ring never visibly drop" is factually wrong.** I cropped the struck right-hand bumper across all eight frames of `strips/s02-bumpers` (`desktop/s02-bumpers-0` to `-7`):
  - In frame 0 the cap is up, with dark chrome skirt showing below it.
  - In frames 1–3 the cap sits about 12–15 px lower, its face is lit, and the teal body glows under it.
  - In frames 4–7 the cap height varies and the skirt reappears by frame 7.
  That is a visible drop, flash and recovery. Critic B saw it ("a cap response").
- **Rebut: steam that "changes little" is overstated.** Across the eight `strips/s02-bumpers` panels, the plumes above the bumpers move and change shape. There are two columns over the left bumper in frame 0, a column over the right bumper in frame 3, and different wisps again in frame 4. They are thin and streaky, but they are not static.
- **Rebut: "no anticipation" on the flipper is the wrong standard.** In `strips/s01-flip` the left flipper goes from rest (frame 2) to fully up (frame 3) in one step. A real flipper's stroke is a few tens of milliseconds, so a one-frame snap at a sampling of "a few sim ticks" is what a correct flipper looks like. Anticipation belongs to events like a kickout or a mode start, not to a flipper. Small correction: the ball then travels up the centre and to the right, towards the Mountain Tower arrow (frames 3–7), not "up the right side".
- **Concede.**
  - There is no ball streak, and nothing else reacts in `strips/s01-flip`.
  - No sling kick is captured.
  - `strips/s03-multiball` cuts off both the top (bumpers half out of frame in `desktop/s03-multiball-4`) and the flippers at the bottom. Both critics are right, and the "whole field" claim in the brief is not met.
- **Score.** With the drop actually visible, Critic A's main reason for 6.0 falls away. I propose 6.5.

### UI: proposed 7.0 (A 6.5, B 7.0, mean 6.75)
- **Concede: the phone chip.** During Derby Day the phone chip shows only "1,274,560" (`mobile/r07-mode`). The brief's claim of a progress and timer chip is not visible there. Note, though, that in normal play the chip does carry progress: "Modes 2/6 · Tower 1/3" (`mobile/r03-play`).
- **Concede: dead space.** The desktop right panel has about 200 px empty above the DMD and below Menu (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **Rebut: the score "flipping between frames" is a convention, not a defect.**
  - When the DMD shows only the score (`desktop/r03-play`), the HUD line holds "Ball 1 of 3".
  - When the DMD is busy with a message, the score moves to the HUD: "SPA CITY / BALL 1" in `desktop/r04-bumpers`, the Arlington icon in `desktop/r06-multiball`, the horse in `desktop/r07-mode`, the steaming mountain in `desktop/r08-wizard`.
  Real DMD machines behave this way, and the score is always on screen.
- **For the 7 anchor.** The 7 anchor is "a DMD with character and readable animations, a clear HUD, menus in the same deco style, nothing overlapping". Against that:
  - The DMD has a pixel-art icon per mode, and the desktop shows "Derby · 18/100 · 45s" (`desktop/r07-mode`).
  - Help, Scores and Title share the gold-and-plum card, and Help now has its "Scroll for more" cue (`desktop/r09-help`).
  - Nothing overlaps in any frame I opened.
- **Score.** One phone defect is not enough to drop below the anchor.

### Typography: proposed 6.5 (A 6.5, B 6.5, mean 6.5)
- **Concede.** Playfield labels are about 5–6 px at desktop gameplay scale (`desktop/r03-play`) and tiny at 390 px (`mobile/r03-play`). They are crisp in the close-ups (`desktop/c01-row`).
- **Credit both critics gave.** The display slab with neon glow is deliberate (`desktop/r01-title`). The DMD dot font is clean (`mobile/r03-play`). Help has a clear hierarchy (`desktop/r09-help`).
- **Score.** Hold at 6.5.

### Readability: proposed 7.0 (A 6.5, B 6.5, mean 6.5)
- **Rebut: the ball measurement, a factual error.** Critic A measures the `desktop/r06-multiball` balls as "about 12 px dots". My crops show each ball at about 17 px across, with a bright specular cross and a dark chrome rim. They are distinct against:
  - the dark wood of the arch, for the top ball at (608,43);
  - the terrazzo between the flippers, at (626,707);
  - the plunger lane, at (826,703).
  The ball in `desktop/r03-play` (about 690,553) is the same size.
- **Rebut: balls "compete with bright silver rails" (Critic B).** In `desktop/r06-multiball` the rails are thin lines 2–3 px wide. The balls are filled discs with a highlight, so they don't read as the same kind of shape.
- **Rebut: the tower arrow placement (Critic B).** In a crop of `mobile/r05-ramp`, the yellow Tower arrow points up and to the right, directly at the ramp's entry flap about 40 px away. Its dot trail leads into it. The cue is aimed at the shot.
- **Evidence the low scores underweight.**
  - Lit and unlit lamps are clearly different on both viewports: warm against olive bathhouse plates in `desktop/c01-row`, purple and gold against dim in `desktop/r07-mode` and `mobile/r07-mode`.
  - The ball reads through the clearer ramp (`desktop/r05-ramp`, `mobile/r05-ramp` at about 650,705).
  - Every shot has its own coloured arrow: blue left orbit, red Vapors, red lock, green Reserve St, yellow Tower. Each lights when the shot is live (`desktop/r08-wizard`).
- **Concede.** Unlit arrows are dim in normal play, and labels can't be read at desktop scale.
- **Score.** I propose 7.0 at moderate confidence. That is +0.5 on the mean, and it mostly removes the weight Critic A put on a size the capture doesn't show.

### Mobile: proposed 6.5 (A 6.5, B 6.5, mean 6.5)
- **Concede.**
  - The landscape table in `mobile/r11-landscape` is about 510 of 1688 px wide, with black margins inside its column.
  - Labels there are unreadable.
  - Mode state is missing from the chip (`mobile/r07-mode`).
- **Rebut the cause behind the landscape fix.** In `mobile/r11-landscape` the table already runs almost the full height, from about y=75 to y=770 of 780. A portrait table in a 390 px-tall viewport can only get wider by tilting further. That is what the other build does in `ab/mobile-r11-landscape`, and both critics preferred the less foreshortened build. So Critic A's test ("table spans ≥ 85% of its column") can't be met without undoing a change both critics rewarded. Narrowing the side panels and enlarging the table moderately is realistic. Filling the column is not.
- **Portrait.** Both critics agree it is framed on purpose. In `mobile/r03-play` and `mobile/r06-multiball` the buttons sit on the apron, clear of the flippers.
- **Score.** Hold at 6.5.

### Authenticity: proposed 7.5 (A 7.0, B 7.5, mean 7.25)
- **The Row and landmarks.**
  - `desktop/c01-row` shows the eight bathhouses in the right order, each named, with different silhouettes: the Quapaw dome, Ozark towers and Fordyce skylight as Critic A lists, plus the distinct rooflines noted under Toys.
  - The twin-towered Arlington is in `desktop/c06-arlington`, the lattice Mountain Tower in `desktop/c05-tower`, and the Vapors neon in `desktop/c04-vapors`.
  - `desktop/c02-springs` has "143°F" on the bumper caps, steam, and the Display Spring with its tufa.
- **Copy.** `desktop/r09-help` hedges the facts ("about" and "reportedly" where sources disagree), says the characters are fictional, and gives the 20 April 1832 reservation correctly.
- **Rebut: "generic boxes".** Critic A's reason for stopping at 7.0 is that "the toy bathhouses are generic boxes". The silhouettes in `desktop/c01-row` contradict that. They are simplified, not generic.
- **Score.** 7.5.

## IP
Both critics report clean, and I agree. No text is painted into the backglass (`desktop/r03-play`) or the upper painting (`desktop/c02-springs`). All lettering is applied type. Critic B's unresolved provenance note on the Vapors sign is reasonable. The sign in `desktop/c04-vapors` is an original neon script treatment of a historical place name.

## A/B
The two critics agree on every category except authenticity:
- A wins art, toys, lighting, UI, readability and mobile.
- Animation and typography are ties.
- Authenticity is a tie for Critic A and an A win for Critic B.

I see no reason to dispute any of these. `ab/mobile-r11-landscape` and `ab/desktop-r03-play` clearly show A's cabinet landscape layout and wider desktop table. `ab/strips-s02-bumpers` shows A's warmer spill on the calm pool.
