## Rulings

### Art: 7.0
- **Upper field.** The repaint works. The bumpers stand on calm water with tufa and the cascade behind them (`desktop/c02-springs`), and the brick promenade and railing frame the Row (`desktop/c01-row`).
- **Backglass.** It is in the same hand as the playfield and has no text (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **Lower plaza.** Here the advocate is right against Critic A. The lower plaza is plainer and softer, but it is not anchor-5 "art in rectangles unrelated to the layout": the fan's rays converge on Shoot Again and the drain, and the 2X–5X triangles sit on its centre petals (`desktop/c03-flippers`, `desktop/r03-play`).
- **What keeps it at 7, not higher.**
  - The inserts are bare plates with no painted surround (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The painted lanterns are out of scale with the models (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - The painting is soft at close range (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Verdict.** It meets the 7 anchor on composition, theming and the backglass, and falls short on insert integration.

### Toys: 6.0
- **What reads.** Every landmark reads at a glance, with eight distinct rooflines (`desktop/c01-row`). The bumpers, the lattice tower, the cross-tied wireform and the chrome ball reach the 7 level (`desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/c02-springs`).
- **Where it fails.**
  - The materials clause of the 7 anchor fails visibly. The Vapors scoop walls and the Arlington lock frame are untextured grey slabs, the rubric's anchor-5 wording (`desktop/c04-vapors`, `desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - The ramp has an opaque grey left wall and an entry block (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - The bathhouses stand on tall blank cream plinths (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - The Arlington's windows have ragged black cells (`desktop/c06-arlington`).
  - Wireform posts end in mid-air (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - The flippers are plain white bats (`desktop/c03-flippers`).
- **Weighing the advocates.** The advocate's sling correction is right but small. The prosecutor's extra defects are real and nobody else priced them, so I sit below the critic mean of 6.25.

### Lighting: 6.5
- **What works.**
  - Lamps bloom only where lit (`desktop/c03-flippers`: lit 2X and 3X against dull 4X and 5X).
  - A bumper hit throws a warm pool that decays (`strips/s02-bumpers`).
  - The wizard drops the GI and lights every coloured shot arrow (`desktop/r08-wizard`). That is a modest mood shift, not a full light show.
- **What fails.**
  - No toy is grounded: the Arlington floats on the painted pool with no contact shadow, and the plinths meet the brick without darkening (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c01-row`).
  - Hard white streaks show on a tower leg and a wireform crossbar (`desktop/c05-tower`).
  - A specular hot spot sits on the "143°F" cap lettering in lit frames (`desktop/s02-bumpers-1`).
  - The spinner still flashes white (`mobile/r03-play`).
- **Verdict.** Both critics' 6.5 stands.

### Animation: 6.5
- **What moves.** The flipper snaps cleanly (`strips/s01-flip`). The bumper visibly drops, lights and recovers (`desktop/s02-bumpers-0` to `-7`), which removes Critic A's main reason for 6.0. The steam changes shape modestly across the bumper strip, and the insert chase runs in multiball (`strips/s03-multiball`).
- **What is missing.** There is no sling kick, no ball streak and no camera reaction anywhere. The multiball strip clips the arch and flippers, so the claimed framing fix is not delivered (`strips/s03-multiball`).
- **Verdict.** The first half of the 7 anchor is met; the follow-through and camera are not.

### UI: 6.5
- **What works.** The DMD has real character at mode starts: the horse, the Arlington and the steaming mountain (`desktop/r07-mode`, `desktop/r06-multiball`, `desktop/r08-wizard`). The menus share the deco card (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r10-scores`).
- **What fails.**
  - Between mode starts the DMD idles on "SPA CITY / BALL 1" through bumper and ramp play (`desktop/r04-bumpers`, `desktop/r05-ramp`), so "readable animations" covers only mode starts.
  - The phone chip drops the mode state during a mode (`mobile/r07-mode`).
  - The desktop right panel has about 200 px empty bands (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **Score placement.** I accept the advocate's point that moving the score between the DMD and the HUD is DMD convention, not a defect.
- **Verdict.** The prosecutor's idle-DMD finding is the decider: below 7.

### Typography: 6.5
- **What works.** The neon slab display face and the DMD dot font are deliberate and clean (`desktop/r01-title`, `mobile/r03-play`).
- **What fails.**
  - The buttons and the score table use a generic sans, so the anchor's "deliberate UI face" is not met (`desktop/r01-title`, `desktop/r10-scores`).
  - Playfield labels are about 5 px at desktop play scale (`desktop/r03-play`).
- **Verdict.** The prosecutor's 6.0 over-weights a UI face that is plain but tidy.

### Readability: 6.5
- **The advocate's two corrections hold.** The balls are about 17 px (`desktop/r06-multiball`), and the tower arrow sits at the ramp mouth (`desktop/r08-wizard`).
- **What works.** Lit and unlit inserts are clearly different (`mobile/r07-mode`).
- **What keeps it below 7.**
  - In normal play the unlit shot arrows are dark (the blue left-orbit and green Reserve St arrows in `desktop/r03-play`), so shot entrances are not obvious.
  - The ball on the ramp is a faint dot against clear plastic and silver tubes (`desktop/r05-ramp`).
  - The "MOUNTAIN TOWER" plate is far from its shot (`desktop/r03-play`).
  - Labels can't be read at play scale (`desktop/r03-play`).

### Mobile: 6.5
- **Portrait.** It is framed on purpose, with a large DMD and buttons on the apron clear of the flippers (`mobile/r03-play`, `mobile/r07-mode`).
- **Landscape.** It is now a deliberate cabinet layout, but the table spans about 510 of 1688 px with black gutters on both sides of it, and the DMD's "BALL 1" line is dim (`mobile/r11-landscape`).
- **Missing state.** The phone has no mode state during a mode (`mobile/r07-mode`).
- **The advocate's caution is right.** Filling the column would need more tilt. The gutters between the table and the side panels are the real waste.

### Authenticity: 7.5
- **What earns it.**
  - The eight bathhouses are named, in order and individually shaped (`desktop/c01-row`).
  - The twin-towered Arlington, the lattice Mountain Tower and the Vapors neon are all present (`desktop/c06-arlington`, `desktop/c05-tower`, `desktop/c04-vapors`).
  - There are 143°F springs and a tufa cascade (`desktop/c02-springs`).
- **The prosecutor's objections are fair stylisation notes, not a drop below 7.5.** The Arlington sits in the middle of the Row, and Buckstaff is plain.

No IP gap.
