# Advocate: loop 4

Capture paths are relative to `games/Pinball/screenshots/review-loop-4/`. I opened every capture cited below at full size. Where I cropped or measured, I say so.

## Summary
Most of what the critics say holds up, and I concede it: no visible mood darkening in the frame captures, a skill-shot lane that barely stands out, clipped "MOUNTAIN TOW" and "PERIC" labels, slingshots with no rubber, box-like buildings, and a small landscape table. The critics overreach in four places:
- Codex says the bumper interiors are "conspicuously faceted". They aren't.
- Critic A says the ball shows only an X-shaped env map with no table reflection. The close-up shows table colours in the ball.
- Critic A says the mid-plaza has "nothing designed into it". The deco fan and arc bands are visible there.
- Codex blames the lower field's art when the lower field is the best-composed zone.

There's also one cause to sharpen. My own crops confirm Critic A's finding that the mood frames have a brighter mean. But a lamp-free patch of the field holds flat at a median luma of 68 to 70 in every state. So the GI is never scaled down, and the extra brightness is lamp spill. The darkening gap stands as written, and its test should measure an unlit patch rather than the frame mean.

Proposed total **60.0 / 90**. Critic A gave 58.5, Codex 60, so the critic mean is 59.25.

## Category by category

### Art: critics 6.5 / 6.5. Proposed 6.5
- **Concede** (A): the upper painting and the backglass are in different hands. "PERIC HALE" is visible in `desktop/c01-row.png`: SUPERIOR is hidden behind the Vapors guide.
- **Rebut, overstated** (A, "middle plaza … flat terrazzo … nothing designed into it"). In `desktop/r03-play.png`, under the lock and behind the SPA CITY logo, there's a blue and terracotta deco fan. Concentric arc bands frame the Valley of the Vapors mode arc. The plaza is sparse, but it is designed.
- **Rebut, in part** (A, "upper third … isn't composed around the shots"). In `desktop/r03-play.png` and `desktop/c01-row.png`, the painted terrace balustrade curves in the same arc as the bathhouse row and the Arlington lock. The grotto and waterfall sit under the S·P·A lanes at the head of the table, which is the spring source on theme. A is right that nothing in the painting points to the orbit or the ramp mouth.
- **Rebut** (Codex G-lower-art, "broad lower paving and generic fan … lack authored specificity"). In `desktop/c03-flippers.png` the fan's arches frame the 2X–5X inserts, and the SHOOT AGAIN star sits at the fan's centre. Critic A calls this "the strongest art on the table", and the capture agrees with A.
- **Concede** (A): CENTRAL AVENUE is cut in `desktop/c04-vapors.png`. That's only in the close-up, where the white harlequin block at upper left occludes it. In play view (`desktop/r03-play.png`, left orbit) the label is whole.

### Toys: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed 6.5
- **Concede** (A, Codex): the bathhouses and the Arlington are clean extrusions with flat window textures and weak grounding (`desktop/c01-row.png`, `desktop/c06-arlington.png`). The slingshots have no rubber, posts or kicker (`desktop/c03-flippers.png`). The ramp floor has a seam and kink at the bend, and the wall tops show ladder-like ticks. White emissive bars on the wireform glare (`desktop/c05-tower.png`).
- **Factual error** (Codex, "conspicuously faceted bumper interiors", citing `desktop/c06-arlington.png`). In both `desktop/c06-arlington.png` and `desktop/c02-springs.png` the teal bodies are smooth translucent cylinders that glow from inside. There are no visible facets.
- **Rebut, in part** (A, "brass caps read as full cylinders"). It's partly fair: from the review angle the brass reads as round tubes on walnut. But Codex calls the brass "more convincing than the architecture", and `desktop/c04-vapors.png` shows the walnut faces under the brass with specular streaks along the tube.
- **Evidence the 6.0 under-credits.** In `desktop/c05-tower.png` the lattice tower has an octagonal cab, and the wireform has cross-ties and chrome supports. The c02 and c06 close-ups show convincing translucent teal bumper bodies, and `desktop/c03-flippers.png` shows clean flippers with red rubber and chrome pivots. That's more than "recognisable models in flat materials", which is the 5 anchor. 6.5 is within +0.25 of the mean.

### Lighting: critics 6.5 / 6.5. Proposed 6.5
- **Concede** (A, Codex): there's no visible mood darkening in the frame captures. My crops of the desktop table region give mean luma r03 73.9, r06 77.5, r07 77.5, r08 80.4; on phone the full frame goes 64.4 to 72.5. The direction matches A's numbers.
- **Cause, sharpened.** A lamp-free patch left of the left slingshot in the desktop frames has a median of 70 / 70 / 68 / 68 across r03, r06, r07 and r08. The phone's lower-left patch is 79 in all four. The GI is never scaled down. The rise in the mean comes from lit arrows, S·P·A lamps and bathhouse spill (the garden and plaza patches rise by 3–7 points). A's fix is right, but his test (frame mean ≤ 0.6× r03) would penalise the very lit inserts that are meant to carry the light. Test an unlit patch instead.
- **Concede** (Codex): white base hotspots at the bumpers and on scoop rims (`desktop/c02-springs.png`, `desktop/c04-vapors.png`).
- **Credit** (both): in `strips/s02-bumpers.png` the interior bumper glow, with caps glowing orange through the steam symbol in frames 0–1, is real bloom. `strips/s03-multiball.png` shows a dark table carried by its inserts.

### Animation: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed 6.5
