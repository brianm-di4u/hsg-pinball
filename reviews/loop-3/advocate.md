# Advocate: loop 3

Paths are relative to `games/Pinball/screenshots/review-loop-3/`. I re-opened every capture cited below and, where a
critic made a measurable claim, re-measured it on the PNG (crop centres and box means, 1280×800 desktop frames,
playfield box x 460–830, y 30–740).

## Summary

The critics broadly agree, and most of their hard findings stand: the olive rails and their overlapping end caps, the
missing flipper decal, the cropped landscape view, the wizard that gets brighter instead of darker, illegible
playfield lettering at the player camera, and objective lines frozen mid-word. I concede all of these. However, Critic A's
readability and toy sections contain several factual errors, and they inflate two gaps (skill-shot lamp and upper-field
ball contrast). Measured on the captures, both gaps already meet or nearly meet Critic A's own pass tests. Critic A
also calls the bumper ring, the wireform posts and the Vapors sign lettering absent, but all three are visible in the close-ups. Codex's
shot-hierarchy and hardware gains are pitched too high given the colour-coded lit arrows in `desktop/r08-wizard.png`
and the posts already on the wireform in `desktop/c05-tower.png`. I propose **59.5 / 90** (critic mean 57.75).

## Facts the critics got wrong

1. **Critic A, `mobile/r02-serve`: "S and A both look lit while the DMD says LANE A".** This is wrong. In a 3× crop of the S·P·A lamps,
   S and P are both dull olive and only A is bright yellow. One lamp is lit, and it matches the DMD's SKILL SHOT: LANE A.
2. **Critic A, `desktop/r02-serve`: "A only marginally brighter".** This is overstated. The 5×5 lamp-centre means are S 107/82/23,
   P 107/81/21 and A 200/156/45, so A is about 1.9× the others. What's missing is a bloom halo, not lamp contrast.
3. **Critic A, `desktop/r04-bumpers`: "ball is a low-contrast grey disc".** This is wrong by Critic A's own test. The ball sits upper-left
   of the bumpers at about (595, 73). Its core luminance is about 179 against a ring average of about 59, a ratio of roughly 3.0,
   which beats the "≥ 2.5" pass condition in G-upper-ball-contrast. In the crop the ball is a silver sphere with a ring highlight,
   clearly separated from the foliage.
4. **Critic A, `desktop/c02-springs`: bumpers have "no skirt, ring".** The ring is wrong. Each bumper stands in a polished chrome base
   ring with a dark collar, visible on all three in `desktop/c02-springs.png` and `desktop/c06-arlington.png`. The
   translucent-body criticism is fair.
5. **Critic A, `desktop/c05-tower`: wireform has "no cross-ties or posts".** The posts are wrong. Dark vertical posts stand at
   regular intervals along the chrome tubes, about six of them between the ramp top and the tower base. Cross-ties are indeed absent.
6. **Critic A, `desktop/c04-vapors`: the sign's "lettering can't be read".** This is overstated. In a 4× crop the pink-outlined blade
   carries teal neon reading V-A-P-O-R-S, set vertically. It's small and turned edge-on, but it isn't blank. The playfield plate
   "THE VAPORS" is plainly legible beside the scoop in both `desktop/c04-vapors.png` and `desktop/c01-row.png`.
7. **Critic A, `mobile/r02-serve`: MOUNTAIN TOWER "collides with its arrow".** The cause is wrong. In a 3× crop the N is cut off by the
   COASTER mode plate overlapping the label. The arrow only touches the R at the far end. The fix is moving the label
   away from the COASTER insert, not from the arrow.
8. **Critic A, `desktop/c03-flippers`: "add a real rubber ring".** The red band sandwiched in the bat's edge is the rubber ring.
   It wraps the whole bat in the 2× crop. The decal criticism stands.
9. **Critic A, `desktop/r03-play`: "desktop still shows the score twice".** This is true only of the idle DMD. In `desktop/r04-bumpers.png`
   the DMD shows SPA CITY / BALL 1, and in `r06`, `r07` and `r08` it shows the mode silhouettes. The HUD carries the only score
   in four of the five desktop play frames.

## Category by category

### Art: critics 6.5 / 7.0 (mean 6.75). Proposed **7.0**
- **Concede.** The upper garden painting is a general scene laid under the bumpers rather than composed around each
  entrance (`desktop/c02-springs.png`). Insert lettering is small at the player camera (`desktop/r03-play.png`).
- **Rebut: "large empty terrazzo areas between the slings and the mode inserts" (Critic A).** In `desktop/c03-flippers.png`
  that zone is the deco fan: blue and red petals with gold inlay radiating up from SHOOT AGAIN, carrying the
  2X–5X inserts. It's a composed, layout-driven motif, not empty fill. The brick terrace wall also curves in a concentric
  arc behind the Row and LOCK (`desktop/c01-row.png`), framing that shot. Codex reads the lower field the same way
  ("composed around the flippers rather than merely filled").
- The backglass in `desktop/r03-play.png` echoes the table: a twin-towered Arlington, a domed bathhouse, the lattice tower on the ridge and steam.
  The rubric's "backglass in the same hand" is met. The 7 anchor is substantially met, and the 9-level "art directs the eye" is not.

### Toys and materials: critics 6.0 / 6.0 (mean 6.0). Proposed **6.0**
- **Concede.** The rails are matte olive cylinders whose corners overlap with visible end caps (`desktop/c04-vapors.png`,
  `desktop/c06-arlington.png`). They read darker than the flat gold slabs in the other build (`ab/desktop-c04-vapors.png`,
  A side). The flipper decal is absent (`desktop/c03-flippers.png`, 2× crop: plain cream top). The ramp walls are stair-stepped, the ramp has a
  seam at the break and no entrance flap (`desktop/c05-tower.png`). The sling base ends show two dark holes (`desktop/c03-flippers.png`).
- **Rebut (partial).** The bumpers have a chrome base ring (fact 4). The wireform has posts (fact 5). The Arlington is
  low-poly but correctly twin-towered with red hipped roofs, matching the backglass building (`desktop/c06-arlington.png`).
  "Toy castle" undersells a recognisable massing. Codex's "huge dark guide walls" is partly a matter of taste. Tall wood or metal guides are
  normal on real machines, and the problem is their dull caps, not their height.
- Net: the strengths (eight distinct façades, lattice tower with lit cab, chrome ball in `desktop/c03-flippers.png`) and
  the confirmed material failures balance at 6.

### Lighting: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed **6.5**
- **Concede, measured.** Critic A's means reproduce exactly: playfield R/G/B 94/72/59 (luminance 75.5) in `desktop/r03-play.png`
  and 103/83/65 (85.9) in `desktop/r08-wizard.png`. `desktop/r07-mode.png` measures 84.7 and `desktop/r06-multiball.png` 82.1. Mood frames are 9–14 %
  *brighter*, not dimmer. The brief's "wizard dims key and fill" is not visible. The bumper bases carry hot white
  specular points (`desktop/c02-springs.png`).
- **Rebut: "mood barely moves" (Critic A).** It moves, just the wrong way. Derby and the wizard are measurably warmer
  (R rises from 94 to 103–106) and brighter, and in `desktop/r08-wizard.png` every shot arrow, the S·P·A lamps and 2X–5X glow with
  bloom while unlit inserts stay dark. Bloom is confined to lit lamps (`desktop/c03-flippers.png`: 2X and 3X bloom, 4X and 5X don't),
  and toys sit on contact shadows (`desktop/c06-arlington.png`). The blue rim flare is gone (`ab/desktop-c06-arlington.png`).
  Most of the 7 anchor is present. The dark-room mood is the miss, and it's captured in G-mood-lighting.

### Animation: critics 6.5 / 6.5 (mean 6.5). Proposed **7.0** (low confidence)
- **Concede.** The rings in `strips/s02-bumpers.png` read as orange annuli, not light. There's no motion trail or flipper rebound in
  `strips/s01-flip.png`.
- **Rebut: what the strips show against the 7 anchor.** Flippers snap: `strips/s01-flip.png` goes from down in frame 2 to fully up in frame 3,
  with the ball leaving up the centre by frame 5. Bumpers flash and drop: in `strips/s02-bumpers.png` the caps glow bright
  green in frames 0–4 and return to dark teal in frames 5–7, while rings expand and fade with decaying alpha over frames 4–7.
  Steam rises: grey puffs drift upward above the bumpers across all eight frames of `strips/s02-bumpers.png`. Light shows run:
  `strips/s03-multiball.png` sequences bumper rings in frames 0–2, Row lamps in frames 1–3 and a purple insert sweep in frames 4–6. A ball also visibly
  travels up the right side in frames 5–7, contradicting "no ball-launch is visible". Four of the five 7-anchor items are shown, and the fifth
  (camera reacting to nudges) is not captured either way.

### UI, DMD and HUD: critics 6.5 / 7.0 (mean 6.75). Proposed **6.5**
- **Concede.** Objective lines freeze mid-word: "RAMPS AND ORBI" in `desktop/r07-mode.png` and `mobile/r07-mode.png`, "RAMP FOR JACKP"
  in `desktop/r06-multiball.png`, "L SHOTS LIT ·" in `mobile/r08-wizard.png`. The A/B shows this is a regression from scrolling:
  the other build printed the whole objective, "RAMPS AND ORBITS: WIN THE RACE" (`ab/desktop-r07-mode.png`, A). Help copy sits
  under the pinned Back button (`mobile/r09-help.png`: "what's lit, or pays a mystery award" cut by the button's top edge). The rubric's
  "nothing overlapping" is not met, which keeps this at 6.5 despite strong DMD character.
- **Rebut.** The score duplication is limited to the idle DMD (fact 9). The menus share one gold-framed deco card with a slab
  heading and gold primary button (`desktop/r01-title.png`, `desktop/r10-scores.png`). The horse, twin-tower and valley silhouettes
  (`desktop/r07-mode.png`, `desktop/r06-multiball.png`, `desktop/r08-wizard.png`) do tell each mode's story at a glance.

### Typography: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed **6.5**
- **Concede.** Playfield labels (VALLEY OF THE VAPORS, CENTRAL AVENUE, THERMAL SPRINGS, the Row plates) are roughly 5 px at
  the player camera in `desktop/r03-play.png`. The MOUNTAIN TOWER label is clipped (`mobile/r02-serve.png`, cause per fact 7). The About
  paragraph is small grey on navy (`desktop/r09-help.png`).
- **Rebut.** The display, UI and DMD roles are deliberate and consistent: neon slab title (`desktop/r01-title.png`), slab
  headings over a clean sans in tables (`desktop/r10-scores.png`), a period serif on the apron cards (`mobile/r11-landscape.png`) and a clean
  dot font on the DMD. Plates are crisp at close range (`desktop/c03-flippers.png` SHOOT AGAIN; `desktop/c01-row.png` QUAPAW, OZARK,
  BUCKSTAFF, LAMAR). Critic A calls the body face the "system sans", but nothing in the capture establishes that.

### Readability: critics 6.0 / 6.5 (mean 6.25). Proposed **6.5**
- **Concede.** The skill lane has no bloom halo (`desktop/r02-serve.png`). The inlanes and outlanes carry no lamp inserts (`mobile/r02-serve.png`:
  the lanes only have white dashes). The bumper-base white highlights are similar in size and tone to a ball near the arch (`desktop/r06-multiball.png`,
  top ball at about (605, 45) next to the bumper speculars).
- **Rebut.** Facts 1–3: the phone shows exactly one lit S·P·A lamp, the desktop lit lamp is about 1.9× its neighbours, and the
  "lost" ball in `desktop/r04-bumpers.png` has a contrast of about 3:1. Shot entrances are colour-coded arrows placed at their mouths: blue at the
  left orbit, pink at the Vapors, red at LOCK, green at Reserve St, yellow at the Mountain Tower ramp. In `mobile/r08-wizard.png` all five glow
  and read instantly at 390 px. All three multiball balls are findable in `desktop/r06-multiball.png` (Critic A agrees).

### Mobile: critics 6.0 / 5.5 (mean 5.75). Proposed **6.0**
- **Concede.** Landscape shows only the table from the SPA CITY logo down, and the bumpers, Row, lock and lanes are off-screen
  (`mobile/r11-landscape.png`). The ☰ button overlaps the left apron card (`mobile/r07-mode.png`, `mobile/r08-wizard.png`).
  Help text meets the Back button (`mobile/r09-help.png`).
- **Rebut (weight).** Portrait, which covers ten of the eleven phone frames, is framed on purpose. The full-width DMD reads at 390 px,
  the chip swaps between progress and score without duplication (`mobile/r02-serve.png` Modes 0/6 · Tower 0/3; `mobile/r07-mode.png` score while the
  DMD holds the callout), the whole table fits, and the controls stay on the apron, clear of the ball's area. One broken orientation pulls the score below 7 but
  doesn't justify 5.5.

### Authenticity: critics 7.0 / 7.5 (mean 7.25). Proposed **7.5**
- **Concede.** The Vapors model is a plain white block (`desktop/c04-vapors.png`). The Superior label is hidden behind the neon blade
  (`desktop/c01-row.png`: only "…HALE" shows).
- **Rebut.** The Vapors is identified by its neon VAPORS blade and the legible THE VAPORS plate (fact 6). The Arlington is twin-towered, as the
  real hotel is (`desktop/c06-arlington.png`). Evidence neither critic credited: the high-score initials are bathhouse abbreviations
  (FDY, QPW, OZK, LMR, HLE, SUP in `desktop/r10-scores.png`), and the DMD uses a horse and rider for Derby Day and twin towers for the Arlington
  (`desktop/r07-mode.png`, `desktop/r06-multiball.png`). The Row order is correct (`desktop/c01-row.png`), and the help text hedges facts ("about",
  "reportedly", `desktop/r09-help.png`).

## A/B

I agree with both critics that B is the better build overall: round rails and bolt heads (`ab/desktop-c04-vapors.png`), silhouette DMD (`ab/desktop-r07-mode.png`).
There is one regression inside B's UI win: the scrolling objective line, where A showed the full sentence. Separately, B's rails are dimmer gold than A's slabs.
